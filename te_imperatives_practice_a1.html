Exercise 2 – Match the Imperative with the Situation
The worksheet prints two pictures both labeled "d." (toy box and rain cloud) instead of unique letters "c." and "d." — a printing error. Matches below use the picture content, not the mislabeled letters.
  • 1. Don't touch that. → e (no-touch symbol)
  • 2. Please be quiet. → a (shush gesture)
  • 3. Take your umbrella. → rain cloud picture (printed as "d.")
  • 4. Turn on the light. → b (light switch)
  • 5. Put your toys away. → toy box picture (also printed as "d.")

Exercise 4 – Read and Follow (signs)
  • 1. No smoking sign → Don't smoke.
  • 2. Trash/litter sign → Throw away your trash. / Use the bin.
  • 3. Caution/slippery sign → Be careful. / Watch your step.
  • 4. Wash hands sign → Wash your hands.
  • 5. No phones sign → Don't use your phone.
Exercise 5 – Rewrite These Sentences Using Imperatives
Items 2 and 4 are already imperative sentences — accept them unchanged, minus "please" if the student simplifies it.
  • 1. You should drink water. → Drink water.
  • 2. Please don't be late. → Don't be late. (already imperative)
  • 3. You should listen to your teacher. → Listen to your teacher.
  • 4. Don't forget to call me. → Don't forget to call me. (already imperative)
  • 5. You should keep your room clean. → Keep your room clean.

Exercise 7 – Choose the Best Imperative
1. Friend to be quiet → a
2. Someone to open the door → a
3. Sister to help you → a
4. People to not use phones → a
5. Brother to clean his room → a
Exercise 8 – Correct the Mistakes
  • 1. Don't to run in the classroom. → Don't run in the classroom.
  • 2. Please don't talking during the test. → Please don't talk during the test.
  • 3. Turn off the lights, please you. → Turn off the lights, please.
  • 4. Don't forgets your homework. → Don't forget your homework.
  • 5. Wash your hands after use the restroom. → Wash your hands after using the restroom.
Exercise 9 – Write Imperatives for These Situations (open)
Accept any correct imperative. Suggested models:
1. Take out the trash. 2. Study hard. 3. Be careful. 4. Stop shouting. 5. Set the table.
Exercise 10 – Free Writing (open, 5 sentences)
Accept any 5 correct imperative sentences. Suggested model:
Brush your teeth every night. Don't litter in the park. Always say thank you. Drink plenty of water. Be kind to others.
Teacher Tip
This is a comprehensive review worksheet — use it after Grammar, Vocabulary, Reading and Writing. For Ex 2, tell students in advance about the duplicate "d." label so nobody worries they made a mistake. For Ex 8, do item 1 on the board together to model the error-correction process before students work independently.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ Don't to run.
✓ Don't run. (no "to" after don't)
✗ Please don't talking.
✓ Please don't talk.
✗ Turn off the lights, please you.
✓ Turn off the lights, please.
✗ Don't forgets your homework.
✓ Don't forget your homework.
Teacher Extra Activity
Classroom Rules Poster (15 min)
In small groups, students design a classroom rules poster using at least 6 imperatives (3 affirmative, 3 negative), illustrated with simple drawings or icons like the ones in Ex 4. Groups present their poster to the class.
