Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Complete the Sentences (am / is / are)
1. I am a student.
2. She is from Canada.
3. They are my friends.
4. He is tall.
5. We are happy today.
6. This is my dog, Max.
Exercise 2 – Choose the Correct Answer (circle am / is / are)
1. I am eight years old.
2. You are very kind.
3. My parents are at work.
4. It is a beautiful day.
5. We are ready.
6. Sarah is a teacher.
Exercise 3 – Rewrite the Sentences (using the word in parentheses)
  • 1. I am a student. (he) → He is a student.
  • 2. She is my sister. (they) → They are my sisters.
  • 3. You are from Spain. (I) → I am from Spain.
  • 4. We are happy. (she) → She is happy.
  • 5. It is a cat. (they) → They are cats.
Exercise 4 – Make Negative Sentences
  • 1. I am not a musician.
  • 2. He is not at home.
  • 3. They are not my neighbors.
  • 4. She is not very busy.
  • 5. We are not from Brazil.
Exercise 5 – Write Questions for the Short Answers
  • 1. Yes, I am. → Are you a student / happy / at home? (accept any)
  • 2. No, she isn't. → Is she from Spain / tall / a doctor? (accept any)
  • 3. Yes, they are. → Are they your friends / at school? (accept any)
  • 4. No, it isn't. → Is it a cat / big / new? (accept any)
  • 5. Yes, we are. → Are we late / friends / ready? (accept any)
Exercise 6 – Write Short Answers
1. Are you a student? → Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
2. Is your name Tom? → Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
3. Are they from Mexico? → Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
4. Is your mother a doctor? → Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
5. Are we late? → Yes, we are. / No, we aren't.
Exercise 7 – Correct the Mistakes
  • 1. I is → I am from Italy.
  • 2. He are → He is my brother.
  • 3. They is → They are happy.
  • 4. She am → She is tired.
  • 5. We is → We are not at school.
Exercise 8 – Describe the Pictures (open)
4 pictures. Accept any correct verb to be sentence describing the image. Suggested models:
Picture 1: They are a family. / She is the mother.
Picture 2: She is a girl. / She is happy.
Picture 3: It is a dog. / The dog is small.
Picture 4: They are students. / They are young.
Exercise 9 – Complete the Dialogue (Alex and Emma)
Hi! My name is Alex. What is your name?
Hello! I am Emma. Nice to meet you!
Nice to meet you, too! Where are you from?
I am from Australia. How about you?
I am from the USA.
Exercise 10 – Write a Short Paragraph (open, 4–5 sentences)
Personal — accept any correct paragraph. Suggested model:
"My name is Carlos. I am 12 years old. I am from Peru. I am a student. My teacher is very kind. I am happy at school."
Teacher Tip
This is a full-practice worksheet — use it as a review after teaching verb to be, not as an introduction. For Ex 7 (Correct the Mistakes), do item 1 together on the board to model the process: "First, find the subject. Then ask: which form of be does this subject need?" For Ex 9 (Dialogue), have pairs perform it aloud after completing it — reading it as a conversation reinforces natural usage.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ I is from Italy. / She am tired.
✓ I am from Italy. / She is tired.
✗ They is happy. / We is not at school.
✓ They are happy. / We are not at school.
✗ Are you a student? — Yes, I am a student.
✓ Yes, I am. (short answer — no repetition)
✗ My name Alex. / I Emma.
✓ My name is Alex. / I am Emma.
Teacher Extra Activity
Error Auction (10 min)
Write 8 sentences on the board — some correct, some with verb to be errors. Groups "bid" points on the ones they think are correct. Reveal which are right. Points deducted for wrong bets. Mixes Ex 7-style mistake correction with team competition.
