Answer Key
Sentence Builder
1. The lamp is on the desk.
2. The shoes are under the bed.
3. The books are on the shelf.
4. The plant is next to the window.
5. The picture is next to the door.
No picture is given for this exercise, so any preposition that makes a logical sentence should be accepted (e.g. "on the door" for item 5 if the picture is a poster).
Look and Write (open, suggested answers)
1. The teddy bear is on the shelf.
2. The backpack is on the door.
3. The shoes are on the floor, next to the bed.
4. The plant is next to the bed.
Complete the Description
My bed is next to the window.
There is a desk next to the bed.
A chair is in front of the desk.
My backpack is on the door.
There is a rug between the bed and the desk.
The last item is the clearest one — "the bed AND the desk" is the classic pattern for "between." The others describe a typical bedroom layout; accept "next to" / "in front of" variations if they are logical.
Write About a Room & Challenge (open)
No fixed answer key — these are personal writing tasks. Use the Writing Checklist to grade: uses prepositions of place, capital letters, full stops, and clear writing.
Teacher Tip
Model one full example on the board before students attempt "Write About a Room" — show how to combine "There is / It is / The..." sentence starters with a preposition of place, using the "About Me" style shown in the worksheet as a guide.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ The rug is between the bed the desk.
✓ The rug is between the bed and the desk.
✗ my backpack is on the door
✓ My backpack is on the door. (capital letter + full stop)
✗ The shoes is under the bed.
✓ The shoes are under the bed.
✗ The desk is next the bed.
✓ The desk is next to the bed.
Teacher Extra Activity
Describe and Draw (10–15 min)
Students swap their "Write About a Room" paragraph with a partner. The partner draws the room based only on the written sentences, then they compare the drawing with what the writer imagined and discuss any missing details.
