Teacher Tip
Encourage students to read the text more than once. Ask them to circle key information (names, places, hobbies) before answering the questions — this helps them locate answers faster and builds active reading habits.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ He have a dog.   ✓ He has a dog.
✗ Daniel are 15 years old.   ✓ Daniel is 15 years old.
✗ His parents is teachers.   ✓ His parents are teachers.
✗ I not am a student.   ✓ I am not a student.
Teacher Extra Activity
Meet My Classmate (10–15 min)
Students write a short paragraph (4–6 sentences) about themselves using the verb to be, following Daniel's text as a model. They should include: name, age, country, school, a pet or hobby. Then they share with a partner and the partner introduces them to the class.
