Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Sentence Builder (put words in the correct order)
  • 1. I am going to visit my grandparents this weekend.
  • 2. She plans to study harder for her exam.
  • 3. We are going to have a party next Saturday.
  • 4. He is not going to watch the game tomorrow.
  • 5. They are going to travel to Colombia in July.
Exercise 2 – Guided Writing (complete Emma's paragraph)
Box: visit · stay · go · study · meet · have · not watch
Hi! My name is Emma. This weekend, I 1) am going to visit my aunt's house in the countryside. I 2) am going to stay there for two days. On Saturday morning, I 3) am going to go hiking with my cousin. In the afternoon, we 4) are going to have lunch with my family. On Sunday, I 5) am going to study my homework and then I 6) am not going to watch a movie in the evening. I'm excited! I 7) am going to meet my cousin again.
Exercise 3 – My Weekend Plans (open — suggested answers)
Students write freely. Accept any going to sentence with correct structure.
On Saturday morning: I am going to study English.
On Saturday afternoon: I am going to visit my friends.
On Saturday evening: I am going to watch a film with my family.
On Sunday: I am going to rest and read a book.
I'm looking forward to spending time with my family.
Exercise 4 – My Vacation Plans (open — suggested model)
Students write 5–6 sentences. Accept any going to paragraph with personal content.
"This summer, I am going to travel to the coast with my family. We are going to stay in a hotel near the beach. I am going to swim and take lots of photos. My brother is going to learn to surf. We are not going to use our phones much. I am really excited about this vacation!"
Exercise 5 – Independent Writing (model paragraph, 6–8 sentences)
"Next year, I am going to start a new English course. I am going to study every day after school. I am going to meet new friends there. I am not going to miss any classes. On weekends, I am going to practice speaking with my classmates. I think it is going to be a great experience!"
Writing Checklist — What to look for
Used going to correctly (subject + be + going to + base verb)
Wrote complete sentences (capital letter + period)
Used time expressions (this weekend, next month, soon...)
Included at least one negative sentence
Checked spelling and punctuation
Teacher Tip
For Ex 2, do the first blank together as a class before students work independently — this reduces blank-page anxiety and ensures they understand the format. For Ex 4 and 5, encourage brainstorming for 2 minutes first (just keywords: beach, family, study, visit) before writing full sentences. Peer correction works well for both open writing tasks.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ I going to visit my aunt.
✓ I am going to visit my aunt.
✗ She is going to stayed there.
✓ She is going to stay there.
✗ We not going to watch a movie.
✓ We are not going to watch a movie.
✗ I am going to meeting my cousin.
✓ I am going to meet my cousin.
Teacher Extra Activity
Class Survey: What Are You Going To Do? (10 min)
Give students a 3×3 grid with activities (study, travel, cook, visit family, go to a concert, rest, take photos, play sports, watch a film). They walk around asking: "Are you going to [activity] this weekend?" and write classmates' names. Then report: "Three students are going to study. Ana is going to travel."
