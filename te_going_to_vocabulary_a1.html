Teacher Tip
Use Ex 1 as a warm-up drill before the rest of the worksheet: show each word, students call out its category (Travel & Places / Activity / Plan / Time). For Ex 3 (Categorizing), have students complete it individually first, then compare in pairs — disagreements on ambiguous words ("explore" = activity or travel?) make for great class discussion at A1 level.

Teacher Extra Activity
Vocabulary Bingo (10–15 min)
Students fill a blank 3×3 grid with 9 words from the worksheet. Call out sentences using going to that include target words ("She is going to take photos at the beach."). Students cross off the word if it appears. First to get a line or full house wins. Great for listening + vocabulary reinforcement.
