Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Match the Words to the Pictures
Items 2/3/6/7 (switch, trash bin, door handle, power button) don't show a clear before/after state, so on/off and open/close are somewhat interchangeable. Best-fit answers below — accept a reasonable alternative if justified.
Window → 1. open
Light switch → 4. turn off
Trash bin → 5. pick up
Hand washing → 7. wash
Boy, hand to ear → 8. listen
Door handle → 2. close
Power button → 3. turn on
Books into box → 6. put away
Exercise 2 – Complete the Chart (3 imperative verbs each, open)
Accept any reasonable imperative per category. Suggested models:
At home: Clean your room. Wash the dishes. Turn off the lights.
At school: Listen to the teacher. Do your homework. Raise your hand.
In the classroom: Sit down. Be quiet. Open your book.
In the kitchen: Wash your hands. Turn off the stove. Put away the dishes.
In the park: Throw away your trash. Keep your dog on a leash. Don't pick the flowers.
Exercise 3 – Find and Circle the Imperatives (word search)
10 target words to find: sit, stand, wait, bring, go, help, quiet, move, share, stop.
Exercise 4 – Choose the Correct Word
1. Open the door, please.
2. Do your homework before TV.
3. Wash your hands before eating.
4. Turn off the lights when you leave.
5. Take your seat and listen.
Exercise 5 – Put the Words in the Correct Group
The box gives 8 words for 3 columns × 3 lines (9 slots). This doesn't divide evenly, and several words (eat, drink) can be DO or DON'T depending on context. Suggested grouping — accept reasonable alternatives:
DO: throw away, recycle, listen
DON'T: eat (in class), talk, run
BE: quiet
"Drink" is left over — it can fit DO ("Drink water") or DON'T ("Don't drink in class") depending on the sentence; accept either placement.
Exercise 6 – Unscramble the Sentences
  • 1. Pick up your toys.
  • 2. Don't run in the class.
  • 3. Listen to the teacher.
  • 4. Clean your room.
  • 5. Turn off the TV.
Exercise 7 – Write Imperatives for These Situations (open)
  • 1. Be quiet.
  • 2. Open the window.
  • 3. Help me.
  • 4. Stop talking.
  • 5. Bring a book.
Exercise 8 – What's the Imperative? (5 pictures, open)
Accept any correct sentence per picture. Suggested models:
1. Eat your food. 2. Be quiet. 3. Wash your hands. 4. Throw away your trash. 5. Turn off the light.
Teacher Tip
For Ex 1, pre-teach the 8 target verbs with gestures before matching — mime each action and have students call out the word. For Ex 5, don't present it as having one fixed answer; use it as a speaking activity where students explain their reasoning to a partner, especially for "eat" and "drink."
Common Student Mistakes

✓ Open the door.

✗ Don't runs in class.
✓ Don't run in class.
✗ Picks up your toys.

Teacher Extra Activity
Charades with Imperatives (10 min)
Write the 8 verbs from Ex 1 on cards. Students take turns miming the action while the class guesses the imperative sentence ("Turn off the light!"). Great physical reinforcement of the target vocabulary.
