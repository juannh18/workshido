3. Find It in the Text

Request: I'd like a vegetable sandwich and an orange juice / I'd like a chicken wrap and a lemonade / We'd like a dessert to share.
Offer: Would you like cheese in your sandwich? / Would you like anything else? / Would you like cream with it?
Preference: I like tomatoes / I don't like cheese / I like iced tea / I don't like chocolate cake.

Your Turn

Open/personal answers — check students use complete sentences with like or would like.

Teaching Support
Teacher Tip
Before reading, ask students to scan the dialogue and underline every instance of like and would like — this primes them to notice the pattern before answering comprehension questions.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ Leo order lemonade.
✓ Leo orders lemonade. / Leo ordered a chicken wrap.
✗ Emma and Leo would like chocolate cake.
✓ Emma and Leo don't want chocolate cake — they choose apple pie.
Extra Class Activity
Order Swap (10 min)

In pairs, students cover the text and try to recall Emma's and Leo's full order from memory, then check against the reading. This reinforces detail retention.

At a Café • Reading • A2
Lesson Plan (35–40 minutes)
Level: A2 Skill: Reading Topic: Like / Would like — preferences, offers and requests Exercises: 3 (1–3) + Your Turn Est. Time: 35–40 minutes
Materials & Prep
Printed worksheet copies (1 per student) Pencils or pens
TimeStageProcedure
5 minWarm-UpAsk: 'What do you usually order at a café?' Elicit a few answers using I like/I'd like.
10 minReadingStudents read The Lunch Order silently, then in pairs read it aloud as Server/Emma/Leo.
8 minTrue or False (1)Students mark T/F and correct the false statements using information from the text.
7 minChoose the Correct Answer (2)Students circle a, b or c for 4 multiple-choice questions.
6 minFind It in the Text (3)Students complete the chart with expressions from the conversation.
4 minYour TurnStudents answer two personal questions in complete sentences.
Follow-Up / Homework Idea

Homework: write a short café order for yourself (3–4 lines) using at least one request and one preference sentence.

For Extra Support

Highlight the four speaker labels (Server, Emma, Leo) in different colors before reading so students can track who says what more easily.

For an Extra Challenge

Students rewrite the dialogue changing the food items, keeping the same request/offer/preference structure.

Extension & Fast-Finisher Ideas
Find one more offer in the text besides 'Would you like anything else?' Write a new preference sentence about a food you don't like. Ask a partner what they would like to eat right now. Underline every polite word (please, thanks) in the dialogue.
Key Language & Board Notes
Target: I'd like (request), Would you like...? (offer), I like/don't like (preference).
Board: I'd like a sandwich, please. Would you like anything else? I like iced tea.
Exit Ticket — Quick Check
  • 1. What did Emma order to drink? → orange juice
  • 2. Why didn't Leo get lemonade? → the café didn't have it
  • 3. What dessert did they share? → apple pie
