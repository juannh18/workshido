Teaching Support
Teacher Tip
Point to the Note box's two mistakes before starting Activity 4 — students who internalize 'doesn't + base verb' and 'Does + base verb' up front make far fewer errors in the Grammar Detective exercise.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ She don't like milk.
✓ She doesn't like milk.
✗ Does Tom works here?
✓ Does Tom work here?
✗ My brother study at night.
✓ My brother studies at night.
✗ We doesn't get up early.
✓ We don't get up early.
Extra Class Activity
Routine Chain (10 min)

Students stand in a circle. The first says one true present simple sentence about their routine; the next repeats it in third person ('He/She gets up at 7') and adds their own. Continue around the circle.

Lesson Plan (35–40 minutes)
Level: A1 Skill: Grammar Topic: Present simple: affirmative, negative and questions Exercises: 4 (1–4) + Exit Ticket Est. Time: 35–40 minutes
Materials & Prep
Printed worksheet copies (1 per student) Pencils or pens
TimeStageProcedure
6 minWarm-UpRead the Quick Guide box together, checking the -s/-es rule and the don't/doesn't distinction.
7 minComplete the Sentences (1)Students write the correct present simple form of the verb in brackets for 6 sentences.
7 minMake Them Negative (2)Students rewrite 4 affirmative sentences in the negative.
7 minBuild the Questions (3)Students order words to form 4 yes/no questions.
7 minGrammar Detective (4)Students find and correct one mistake in each of 4 sentences.
4 minExit TicketStudents write one affirmative, one negative and one question about their own routine.
Follow-Up / Homework Idea

Homework: write 5 sentences describing a family member's daily routine using present simple third person.

For Extra Support

Give a reference card with the Remember box rules (he/she/it → -s, don't/doesn't, do/does) taped to the desk so students can check spelling as they work through Activities 1–4.

For an Extra Challenge

Students write a short paragraph (4–5 sentences) about a classmate's typical school day, using at least one negative and one question.

Extension & Fast-Finisher Ideas
Name two verbs that need -es instead of just -s. Write a negative sentence about something you don't do on weekends. Ask a partner a does-question about their weekend routine. Find and correct one more possible mistake you can think of.
Key Language & Board Notes
Target: Present simple affirmative (verb-s/-es), negative (don't/doesn't + base verb), questions (Do/Does + subject + base verb?).
Board: She gets up at 7. They don't live near the school. Does he walk to school?
Exit Ticket — Quick Check
  • 1. Third-person form of 'study'? → studies
  • 2. Negative of 'They play soccer'? → They don't play soccer.
  • 3. Question with 'she / read'? → Does she read?
