Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Match the Words with the Pictures (1–10)
1. sofa → green sofa picture
2. table → wooden table picture
3. chair → green chair picture
4. bed → bed picture
5. plant → potted plant picture
6. lamp → table lamp picture
7. fridge → fridge picture
8. shelf → bookshelf picture
9. mirror → round mirror picture
10. rug → rug picture
Each word matches the picture directly below its number in the worksheet, in the same order shown.
Exercise 2 – Categorize the Words
Living Room
sofa, rug, picture
Kitchen
fridge, table, oven
Bedroom
bed, lamp, mirror
Bathroom
toilet, shower, sink
Other
plant, chair, shelf (found in more than one room — accept reasonable placement)
Exercise 3 – Complete the Sentences (word from box)
  • 1. There is a sofa in the living room.
  • 2. There are two chairs at the dining table.
  • 3. There is a green plant next to the window.
  • 4. There is a fridge in the kitchen.
  • 5. There is a big bed in the bedroom.
  • 6. There is a rug on the floor.
  • 7. There is a lamp on the table.
  • 8. There is a mirror on the wall.
  • 9. There is a chair near the desk.
  • 10. There is a shelf with books in the bedroom.
"Chair" is used twice (items 2 and 9) — all 10 box words are used at least once.
Exercise 4 – How Many? (there is / there are)
1. There is a sofa.
2. There are two windows.
3. There are three lamps.
4. There are some plants.
5. There are four chairs.
6. There is a shelf.
7. There are two mirrors.
8. There is a fridge.
Exercise 5 – Describe the Picture (kitchen, open)
Accept any 6–8 correct sentences describing the kitchen picture. Suggested model:
There is a kitchen island. There are three pendant lights above it. There is a fridge next to the window. There is a wooden table with chairs. There are some plants on the shelves. There is a window above the sink. There are some pots on the stove.
Exercise 6 – Extra Challenge (personal, 5 sentences)
Open — accept any correct there is/there are sentences about the student's own bedroom.
There is a bed in my bedroom. There is a desk next to the window. There are two lamps. There is a mirror on the wall. There are some books on the shelf.
Teacher Tip
Pre-teach the 10 words in Ex 1 with flashcards or realia before students attempt the matching. For Ex 2, let students discuss "plant," "chair," and "shelf" in pairs — these can reasonably appear in more than one room, which is a great discussion point at A1 level. For Ex 4, remind students the answer depends only on whether the noun is singular or plural, not on the object itself.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ There is two chairs.
✓ There are two chairs.
✗ There are a fridge.
✓ There is a fridge.
✗ There is some plants.
✓ There are some plants.
✗ Is a sofa in the room.
✓ There is a sofa in the room.
Teacher Extra Activity
Furniture Memory Game (10 min)
Show a picture of a furnished room for 30 seconds, then hide it. In pairs, students write as many "There is / There are" sentences as they remember about the room. The pair with the most correct sentences wins. Great recycling of Ex 1–2 vocabulary.

Lesson Plan (45 minutes)
TimeStageProcedure
5 minWarm-UpShow the header living-room photo. Elicit any furniture words students already know in English.
10 minPresentationPre-teach the 10 target words (Ex 1) with flashcards/realia. Students do the matching exercise, then check in pairs.
10 minPracticeStudents complete Ex 2 (categorize) and Ex 3 (sentence completion). Discuss the "Other" category items as a class.
8 minControlled PracticeStudents complete Ex 4 individually (there is/there are with quantities), then self-check against the picture captions.
10 minProductionStudents write their Ex 5 description individually, then read it aloud to a partner who draws the kitchen based only on what they hear.
2 minWrap-UpQuick round: each student says one sentence about their own bedroom (Ex 6) as an exit ticket.
