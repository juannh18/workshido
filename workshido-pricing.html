Frequently asked questions
Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes, absolutely. You can cancel anytime from your account settings. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period, and we won't charge you again.

Every worksheet on Workshido is free to download. Premium unlocks the Teacher Edition for almost every worksheet (new ones are added every week): a complete answer key, a ready-to-use lesson plan, teacher tips, common student mistakes, and an extra activity to do in the classroom.

Yes — you need a free account to download. It only takes a few seconds with Google sign-in, and once you're in, every worksheet is free to download with no limits.
