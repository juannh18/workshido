⚠ Blank 8 — "we had (8) ___ lunch": natural English uses no article with meals ("we had lunch"). Since the exercise forces an article, accept a as the least-bad choice, but use the item to teach the real rule: no article before breakfast / lunch / dinner.
Exercise 2 – Circle the Correct Article (10 items)
  • 1. She is a doctor at a hospital. (also accept the hospital if a specific one is meant)
  • 2. I saw an amazing movie last night.
  • 3. He wants to buy a new laptop.
  • 4. We go to ___ school by bus — see note ⚠
  • 5. There is a book on the table.
  • 6. I have an orange in my bag.
  • 7. They visited the Eiffel Tower.
  • 8. She is an honest person.
  • 9. We had a great time at the party.
  • 10. He found an old key in the drawer.
⚠ Item 4 — "We go to ___ school by bus": natural English is "go to school" with no article. Since only a / an / the are offered, the is the least-bad choice ("we go to the school by bus" = the building). Point out the real rule to the class.
Exercise 3 – Answer the Questions (Suggested Answers)
  • 1. He/She visited an old city (with a friend).
  • 2. They took a train (they traveled by train).
  • 3. They started their day at a famous museum.
  • 4. They had lunch at a small café near the river.
  • 5. It was a beautiful day / a wonderful experience.
Teacher Tip
Teach the "first mention / second mention" trick with the text: the first time we talk about something general we use a/an ("a famous museum"), and when it is specific or already known we use the ("the city center", "the river"). Have students label each blank G (general) or S (specific) before choosing.

Teacher Extra Activity
A / An / The Cards (10 min)
Give each student three cards: A, AN, THE. Read sentences from the worksheet (or new ones) leaving a gap, and students raise the correct card. Instant feedback for you, zero prep, and it re-tests the tricky items (honest, Eiffel Tower, orange).

Materials & Prep
Printed worksheet copies (1 per student) Board and markers Three cards per student (A / AN / THE) for the extra activity Timer or clock for pacing
Lesson Plan (45–50 minutes)
TimeStageProcedure
5 minWarm-UpWrite "___ apple, ___ banana, ___ sun" on the board and elicit the articles. Ask why: vowel sound → an, consonant sound → a, unique/specific → the.
8 minPresentationPresent the three rules with examples: a + consonant sound, an + vowel sound, the + specific noun. Include the tricky cases: an honest person (silent h), the Eiffel Tower (unique landmark).
12 minControlled PracticeStudents read "A Day in the City" and complete the 15 blanks individually. Check as a class, asking "general or specific?" for each blank. Use blank 8 to teach the no-article rule with meals.
8 minGuided PracticeStudents complete Exercise 2 in pairs (circle the article). Review together; discuss item 4 ("go to school") as a special case.
8 minReading & ProductionStudents answer Exercise 3 in complete sentences, reusing articles correctly from the text.
7 minWrap-UpPlay one quick round of A/An/The cards with 5 new sentences to consolidate the lesson.
Follow-Up / Homework Idea
Students write a short paragraph (4–5 sentences) titled "A Day in My City", using at least three a/an and three the, following the structure of the worksheet text. Next class, partners exchange paragraphs and circle every article.
For Extra Support Have weaker students complete only blanks 1–8 of Exercise 1 first and check before continuing. Give them the G/S (general/specific) label strategy in writing at the top of their worksheet.
For an Extra Challenge Ask fast finishers to find the two items on the worksheet where natural English would use no article (blank 8 "had lunch" and item 4 "go to school") and explain the rule to the class in their own words.
