Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Picture Sentences (Suggested Answers)
Open answers — accept any correct sentence using a, an, or the.
  • 1. Laptop → This is a laptop. / The laptop is new.
  • 2. Airport (Departures) → They are at the airport. / There is an airport near my city.
  • 3. Cup of coffee → This is a cup of coffee. / The coffee smells good.
  • 4. Mountain lake → There is a lake in the mountains. / The lake is very clean.
  • 5. Bookshelf → There is a bookshelf with books. / The books are on the shelf.
⚠ Four of the five pictures (laptop, airport, coffee, mountain lake) are almost identical to Exercise 1 of the Articles Grammar 2 worksheet — only the fifth picture changes (bookshelf here vs. living room there). If you use both worksheets with the same group, expect repeated sentences in this exercise.
Exercise 2 – Complete the Paragraph (10 blanks)
1) Today is ___ Saturday — see note ⚠
6) a pastry
2) a new café
7) a bookshop
3) a small place
8) the café
4) a beautiful garden
9) a great bookshop
5) a cup of coffee
10) ___ interesting books — see note ⚠
⚠ Blank 1 — "Today is (1) ___ Saturday": natural English uses no article with days ("Today is Saturday"). If an article is forced, a is the least-bad option. Use it to teach the real rule.
⚠ Blank 10 — "I always find (10) ___ interesting books there": the noun is plural, so a/an is impossible and the sounds unnatural here (natural English: "some interesting books" or no article). If forced, accept the; better, use the item to teach that a/an never goes with plurals.
Exercise 3 – Write About Your Favorite Place (Model Answer)
Guiding questions: Where is it? What can you do there? What makes it special? Useful words: park, restaurant, beach, museum, library, city, place, building.
My favorite place is a park near my house. It is a quiet place with a small lake. I can ride my bike and read a book under the trees. The park is special because I go there with my family every Sunday.
Teacher Tip
Exercise 2 tells one continuous story, so it is perfect for teaching first vs. second mention: the bookshop appears with a in blank 7 and the café returns with the in blank 8. Read the paragraph aloud once before students fill anything in.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ I'm going to an new café.
✓ I'm going to a new café. (next sound is /n/)
✗ a interesting book
✓ an interesting book
✗ I visit a bookshop near a café. (second mention)
✓ ...near the café. (we already know which café)
✗ I always find an interesting books.
✓ a/an never goes with plural nouns.
Teacher Extra Activity
Story Chain (10 min)
Start a story: "Yesterday I found a box." Each student adds one sentence, and every new noun must enter with a/an and return with the ("In the box there was an old map. The map showed a beach..."). Great for first/second mention practice.

Materials & Prep
Printed worksheet copies (1 per student) Board and markers An object in a box for the Story Chain warm-up (optional) Timer or clock for pacing
Lesson Plan (45–50 minutes)
TimeStageProcedure
5 minWarm-UpTell a two-line story on the board: "I have a cat. The cat is black." Ask: why a first and the after? Elicit the first/second mention rule.
8 minPresentationReview the Writing Tip box: one of many → a/an; something specific → the; plural or uncountable → generally no article. Give one example of each.
10 minControlled PracticeRead the Exercise 2 paragraph aloud, then students complete the 10 blanks individually. Check as a class, discussing blanks 1 and 10 as special cases (days and plurals take no article in natural English).
10 minGuided PracticeIn pairs, students write sentences for the five pictures in Exercise 1. Ask each pair to use a/an in one sentence and the in another.
10 minProductionStudents write about their favorite place (Exercise 3) using the guiding questions and the Useful Words box. Circulate and check article use.
5 minWrap-UpPlay one round of Story Chain: every new noun enters with a/an and returns with the.
Follow-Up / Homework Idea
Students describe their favorite place to a family member and then write two extra sentences answering: "When do you go there?" and "Who do you go with?" — underlining every article they use. Review a few examples next class.
For Extra Support For Exercise 3, give weaker students a frame: "My favorite place is a ___ . It is in ___ . I can ___ there. The ___ is special because ___ ." They complete it with the Useful Words box.
For an Extra Challenge Ask fast finishers to rewrite the Exercise 2 paragraph about a different place (a market, a library) keeping every article correct — first mention a/an, second mention the.
