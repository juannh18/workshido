Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Match the Words with Correct Pictures (A–J)
1. student → picture of a student (studying)
2. teacher → picture of teacher at board
3. friend → picture of two people together
4. happy → picture of smiling person
5. tired → picture of person looking sleepy
6. hungry → picture of person with food/stomach
7. tall → picture of tall person
8. short → picture of short person
9. country → picture of globe / map
10. nationality → picture of flag / passport
Accept any clearly correct match based on picture in the worksheet. Picture letters (A–J) depend on the worksheet layout.

2. This is my friend, Tom.
3. She is a very good student.
4. We are happy to be here!

Exercise 5 – Write About Yourself (personal — suggested model)
Accept any grammatically correct sentences using verb to be with personal information.
My name is [name]. I am [age] years old. I am from [country]. I am a student. I am [feeling] today.
Challenge (4 sentences): I am friendly. My teacher is helpful. My friends are funny. We are happy at school.
Teacher Tip
For Ex 1, pre-teach the vocabulary with flashcards before matching — show each image and elicit the word chorally. For Ex 4, let students discuss in pairs whether "tall" and "short" belong in a new category — the discussion itself reinforces the vocabulary. Ex 5 (Write About Yourself) works well as a class sharing activity: each student reads one sentence while others listen and check.

Teacher Extra Activity
Guess Who? (10 min)
A student thinks of a famous person. The class asks yes/no questions using "Is he/she...?" — "Is he from the USA? Is she a singer? Is she happy in the picture?" First to guess correctly wins. Forces natural use of is/are in questions and short answers.
