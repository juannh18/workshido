Answer Key
Text: "A Weekend in Paris" — a blog post about a trip with 16 numbered blanks. Exercise 2 repeats the same 16 items as circle-the-article, so both share one key.
Exercises 1 & 2 – Text Blanks / Circle the Article (16 items)
  • 1. It was an amazing experience!
  • 3. We took a train from London. (the train also acceptable)
  • 5. We went to a small hotel — 6. near a famous museum.
  • 8. at a nice café — 9. I ordered a sandwich — 10. and an orange juice.
  • 11. We visited an old church — 12. walked along the river — 13. enjoyed an ice cream.
  • 15. at a small Italian restaurant — 16. Paris is a wonderful city.
⚠ Item 2 — "On (2) ___ Friday morning": natural English uses no article ("On Friday morning"). If forced, accept a or the.
⚠ Item 4 — "The train ride was (4) ___ comfortable.": there is no noun after the blank, so no article is grammatically possible ("was a comfortable" ✗). This is a flaw in the worksheet — tell students to cross this blank out (or write "very"). In Exercise 2, skip item 4 or accept any answer.
⚠ Items 7 and 14 — "We had (7) ___ lunch" / "we had (14) ___ dinner": meals take no article in natural English. If forced, accept a; use both items to teach the real rule.
Exercise 3 – Answer the Questions (Suggested Answers)
  • 1. The writer went to Paris (for the weekend).
  • 2. He/She went with his/her best friend, Emma.
  • 3. They took a train from London (they traveled by train).
  • 4. They visited an old church, walked along the river, took photos and enjoyed an ice cream.
  • 5. The food was really good (and the pasta was delicious).
  • 6. Yes — because Paris is a wonderful city (he/she can't wait to go back).
Exercise 4 – Vocabulary in Context
1. a person you like and enjoy spending time with → (best) friend
2. a very good or special activity or event → (amazing) experience
3. a place where you can buy and eat food and drinks → café
4. a building used for Christian worship → church
5. food from Italy, such as pasta or pizza → pasta / Italian (food)
Think About It!
Open answer. Example: "Yes, I like traveling to new places because I can see new things and try new food."
Teacher Tip
Exercises 1 and 2 are the same 16 items in two formats. Use Exercise 1 (text) for the first pass in context, then Exercise 2 (circle) as instant self-correction: students compare their circles with their text answers before you give the key.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ It was a amazing experience.
✓ It was an amazing experience.
✗ We visited a old church.
✓ We visited an old church.
✗ We walked along a river. (a specific, known river)
✓ We walked along the river.
✗ Paris is the wonderful city.
✓ Paris is a wonderful city. (one of many)
Teacher Extra Activity
My Dream Weekend (10–15 min)
Students plan a dream weekend trip in pairs (city, transport, hotel, two activities, one meal) and present it in 4–5 sentences imitating the blog post. The class listens and raises a hand every time they hear a/an or the.

Materials & Prep
Printed worksheet copies (1 per student) Board and markers Optional: photos of Paris for the warm-up Timer or clock for pacing
Lesson Plan (45–50 minutes)
TimeStageProcedure
5 minWarm-UpAsk: "What city do you want to visit? What can you see there?" Collect answers and write two on the board, highlighting the articles students use.
8 minPre-ReadingLook at the photo and title "A Weekend in Paris". Predict: Who went? How did they travel? Review quickly: a/an = general, the = specific.
12 minReading & Gap FillStudents read the blog post and complete the 16 blanks (Exercise 1). Then they do Exercise 2 (circle) as self-check. Give the key, using items 2, 4, 7 and 14 to teach when English uses NO article.
10 minComprehensionStudents answer Exercise 3 in complete sentences. Check in pairs, then as a class.
7 minVocabularyStudents complete Exercise 4 (find words in the text) individually. Quick oral check.
8 minProduction & Wrap-UpStudents answer "Think About It!" and share with a partner. Close with 2–3 volunteers telling the class about a place they want to visit.
Follow-Up / Homework Idea
Students write their own mini blog post, "A Weekend in ___" (5–6 sentences), about a real or imaginary trip, using at least four a/an and two the. Next class, they exchange posts and circle every article their partner used.
For Extra Support Let weaker students work on Exercise 2 (isolated sentences) before the full text of Exercise 1 — shorter chunks make the choice easier. Accept short answers in Exercise 3 before expanding them together.
For an Extra Challenge Ask fast finishers to find the three items where natural English would use no article (Friday morning, lunch, dinner) and rewrite those sentences correctly without one.
