Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Grammar Box (Read and Study)
No answers needed. This = one thing near me / That = one thing far from me / These = two or more near me / Those = two or more far from me.
Exercise 2 – Visual Comparison
No answers needed — reference examples: "This apple is red." / "That apple is red." / "These pens are new." / "Those pens are new."
Exercise 3 – Choose the Correct Demonstrative
No near/far cue is shown in the pictures for this exercise (unlike Ex 6). Accept "this/that" for singular items and "these/those" for plural items — students should justify their choice based on the picture.
1. This is my teacher.
2. These are my brothers.
3. This is my backpack.
4. These are my notebooks.
5. This is your water bottle.
6. These are their bicycles.
Exercise 4 – Complete the Sentences
No near/far cue given for these items — singular/plural is clear from the verb, but this/that or these/those both work. Suggested defaults below; accept the alternative if the student justifies it.
1. This is a very nice picture.
2. These shoes are too small.
3. This is my classroom.
4. Those trees are very tall.
5. This is your cat.
6. These are my new markers.
7. That building is a museum.
8. Those books are interesting.
Exercise 5 – Look at the Picture (open, living room)
Accept any 4 correct sentences describing the picture. Suggested model:
1. This is a sofa. 2. This is a coffee table. 3. That is a bookshelf. 4. These are some books.
Exercise 6 – Rewrite the Sentences (near / far given)
  • 1. (near) This is my pencil.
  • 2. (far) Those are my cousins.
  • 3. (near) This is my phone.
  • 4. (far) Those are your bags.
  • 5. (near) These are my keys.
Exercise 7 – Mini Dialogue
Both items in the dialogue (mug, notebooks) are shown near the speaker, so "this/these" fits consistently throughout:
  • A: What's this? — B: This is my favorite mug.
  • A: And what are these? — B: These are my notebooks.
  • A: Where are these? — B: These are on the table.
Teacher Tip
Start with the Grammar Box (Ex 1) and the Visual Comparison (Ex 2) as a physical demonstration: point to objects near you ("this/these") and far away ("that/those") before students open their books. For Ex 3 and Ex 4, where no distance cue is given, use it as a discussion point — ask students to imagine the scene and justify their choice rather than treating it as a single right answer.
Common Student Mistakes

✓ These are my brothers.
✗ Those is my cousins.
✓ Those are my cousins.
✗ These is my pencil.
✓ This is my pencil.
✗ What's these? (mismatched number)
✓ What's this? / What are these?
Teacher Extra Activity
Near and Far Pointing Game (10 min)
Place objects around the classroom — some on students' desks (near) and some across the room or out the window (far). In pairs, students take turns pointing and saying a sentence: "This is a pencil." / "Those are cars." The partner confirms if the demonstrative matches the distance.

Lesson Plan (45 minutes)
TimeStageProcedure
5 minWarm-UpHold up an object near you and one across the room. Ask: "This or that?" Elicit both words before opening the worksheet.
10 minPresentationGo through the Grammar Box and Visual Comparison (Ex 1–2) on the board. Drill chorally with real classroom objects at different distances.
10 minControlled PracticeStudents complete Ex 3–4 individually, then compare answers in pairs — since several items have no explicit distance cue, have pairs discuss and justify their choices.
8 minGuided PracticeStudents complete Ex 5 (picture description) and Ex 6 (near/far rewrite) together in pairs.
8 minProductionPairs complete and perform the Ex 7 mini dialogue aloud, then create their own 4-line dialogue about two classroom objects.
4 minWrap-UpQuick round: each student says one true sentence about the classroom using a demonstrative.
