Answer Key

The worksheet has 10 exercises: odd numbers (1, 3, 5, 7, 9) appear in the left column; even numbers (2, 4, 6, 8, 10) in the right column.

1 Choose the Correct Option — circle am / is / are
1. am — I am going to visit my cousin tomorrow.
2. is — She is going to study for the test.
3. are — They are going to travel to Spain next week.
4. are — We are not going to have a party.
5. is — He is not going to play football today.
6. Are — Are you going to watch the movie tonight?
7. Are — Are they going to arrive at the airport?
8. Is — Is she going to buy a new phone?
9. am — I am going to learn how to cook.
10. are — We are not going to stay home.

  • 1. I am going to study English this evening.

  • 4. He is not going to watch TV tonight.

  • 6. You are going to help me with my homework.
  • 7. My brother is going to play basketball tomorrow.
  • 8. I think it is going to rain later.
5 Read and Answer — Mia's family trip to Canada

Text summary: Mia's family flies to Toronto (3 days) → Niagara Falls. Mia: takes photos. Mom: tries Canadian food. Dad: hikes in the mountains. Brother: does NOT hike — stays at hotel, plays video games.

  • 1. They are going to go to Canada (Toronto and Niagara Falls).
  • 2. They are going to fly (by plane).
  • 3. First, they are going to fly to Toronto and stay for three days.
  • 4. She is going to take lots of photos.
  • 5. He is going to go hiking in the mountains.
  • 6. He is not going to go hiking. (He is going to stay at the hotel.)

6 Complete the Conversation — going to + verbs in brackets
A: What are you going to do this weekend?
B: I am going to visit my grandparents.
A: Really? How are you going to travel?
B: We are going to drive to their house.
A: What are you going to do there?
B: We are going to have a barbecue and play some games.
A: That sounds fun!
8 Choose the Correct Answer — best response
  • 1. Are you going to the movies tonight? → a) Yes, I am.
  • 2. What are your plans for next weekend? → b) I am going to the beach.
  • 3. Is she going to study English? → a) Yes, she is.
10 Writing Task — model answer
"Next month, I am going to visit my cousins in another city. We are going to go to the park and have a barbecue. I am not going to use my phone much. We are going to play some games and watch a movie. I am really excited about this plan!"

Check: going to used correctly • at least one negative • time expressions • capital letters & periods.

Teacher Tip
Use this worksheet for consolidation after the main lesson. Let students work independently through all 10 exercises, then review as a class. For Ex 5 (reading) and Ex 10 (writing), use peer correction: students swap papers and check each other's going to structure before whole-group feedback.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ They going to travel to Spain.
✓ They are going to travel to Spain.
✗ Is she going to visited the airport?
✓ Is she going to visit the airport?
✗ He not is going to buy a jacket.
✓ He is not going to buy a jacket.
✗ I am going to stays up late.
✓ I am not going to stay up late.
Teacher Extra Activity
Fast Finish Challenge (5–10 min)
Write on the board: next summer / your best friend / your family / you and your classmates. Students write one affirmative, one negative, and one question for each subject. Collect as a quick formative assessment for all three going to forms.
