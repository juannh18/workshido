Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Write About This or That (6 pictures, open)
All 6 pictures are close-up product shots with no distance cue, so "this" is the natural default for every item; accept "that" if the student justifies it. Suggested models:
  • 1. This is a pencil.
  • 2. This is a whiteboard.
  • 3. This is a laptop.
  • 4. This is a backpack.
  • 5. This is a clock.
  • 6. This is an armchair.
Exercise 2 – Write About These or Those (6 pictures, open)
All 6 pictures are close-up shots, so "these" is the natural default; accept "those" if justified. Suggested models:
  • 1. These are books.
  • 2. These are shoes.
  • 3. These are pencils.
  • 4. These are plants.
  • 5. These are chairs.
  • 6. These are picture frames.
Exercise 3 – Describe Your Classroom (open, 5–6 sentences)
Accept any correct description of the classroom picture. Suggested model:
This is a whiteboard. This is the teacher's desk. These are the students' desks. That is a bookshelf in the corner. That is a globe on the shelf. This is a plant near the window.
Exercise 4 – Write a Short Paragraph (personal, 6–8 sentences)
Open — accept any grammatically correct paragraph about the student's own room. Suggested model:
This is my bed. It's very comfortable. That is my desk near the window. This is my laptop on the desk. These are my books on the shelf. That is my backpack on the floor. This is a small rug next to my bed. I love my room.
Teacher Tip
Before Ex 1 and Ex 2, remind students of the Writing Tips box: this/that = one thing, these/those = two or more, this/these = near you, that/those = far from you. Since none of the pictures in Ex 1–2 show explicit distance, encourage students to imagine the object is right in front of them (near) unless they have a reason to say otherwise — this keeps answers consistent and avoids random guessing.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ This are my books.
✓ These are my books.
✗ These is a pencil.
✓ This is a pencil.
✗ That is my desk near the window. Are my books on the shelf.
✓ ...These are my books on the shelf. (don't drop the subject)
✗ Those is my backpack.
✓ That is my backpack. / These are my backpacks.
Teacher Extra Activity
Describe and Guess (10–15 min)
In pairs, Student A writes 4 sentences describing objects in their real backpack or pencil case using this/that/these/those, without naming the object directly (e.g., "This is small and blue."). Student B guesses the object. Great for combining vocabulary recall with the target grammar in a low-pressure speaking format.
