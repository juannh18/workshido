Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Circle the Correct Answer (can / can't)
  • 1. I can ride a bike. (personal — accept both; can is expected)
  • 2. Penguins can't fly.
  • 3. Fish can swim.
  • 4. My teacher can speak English.
  • 5. We can't sleep in class.
Exercise 2 – Complete the Sentences (can or can't)
  • 1. A baby can't drive a car.
  • 2. Birds can fly.
  • 3. I can play basketball. (personal — accept both)
  • 4. My grandmother can't use a smartphone.
  • 5. We can read English books.
Exercise 3 – Match (who can / can't do what?)
1. A chef → can → b. cook delicious meals
2. A bird → can → d. fly
3. A fish → can → e. swim
4. A baby → can't → c. drive a car
5. A musician → can → a. play an instrument
Sentences: A chef can cook delicious meals. / A bird can fly. / A fish can swim. / A baby can't drive a car. / A musician can play an instrument.
Exercise 4 – Pair Work (ask and answer — personal)
Students ask each other and write their partner's answers. Accept yes/no with full short answer.
1. Can you swim? → Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
2. Can you cook? → Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
3. Can you ride a bike? → Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
4. Can you play the piano? → Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
5. Can you speak another language? → Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
Exercise 5 – Write About Yourself (5 sentences)
Open — accept any can / can't sentence. Model: I can play soccer.
I can ride a bike.
I can speak English and Spanish.
I can't play the guitar.
I can swim very fast.
I can't drive a car.

Challenge Box (3 can / 3 can't)
Accept any correct can / can't statements about the student's own abilities.
I CAN: 1. play soccer 2. cook pasta 3. ride a bike
I CAN'T: 1. fly a plane 2. play the piano 3. speak Japanese
Teacher Tip
Emphasize the grammar rule from the box: can / can't + base verb (no -s, no -ing, no to). A common mistake is "She cans sing" or "He can to swim." For Ex 1, items 2 and 5 are great for class discussion: "Why can't penguins fly? Why can't we sleep in class?" — it makes the grammar feel meaningful rather than mechanical.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ She cans sing. / He can to swim.
✓ She can sing. / He can swim.
✗ I can't to ride a bike.
✓ I can't ride a bike.
✗ Can she swims? / Can you playing guitar?
✓ Can she swim? / Can you play the guitar?
✗ Yes, I can swim. (long answer)
✓ Yes, I can. (short answer — no verb after can)
Teacher Extra Activity
Abilities Survey (10–15 min)
Students make a 4×4 grid with abilities (swim, cook, sing, ride a bike, speak French, play chess, draw, dance). They walk around asking: "Can you [activity]?" and write the names of classmates who say yes. Then report to class: "Maria can dance and sing. Pablo can't draw but he can swim." Covers can / can't in questions, answers, and third-person statements.
