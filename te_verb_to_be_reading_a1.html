Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Pre-Reading (open answers)
Accept any reasonable prediction about the image/person. Examples:
Who is the person? → He is a student / a young man / a teenager.
Where is he from? → He is from Mexico / Spain / Latin America.
Exercise 2 – Reading Text: "Meet Daniel"
No answers needed — students read the text. Key facts to note: Daniel / 15 years old / Mexico / Greenfield High School / class 10b / only child / parents are teachers / dog: Rocky (small and playful) / good at soccer / fan of Imagine Dragons / happy.
Exercise 3 – Reading Comprehension
A. Choose the Correct Answer
1. Daniel is b. 15 years old.
2. He is from b. Mexico.
3. His school is c. big and modern.
4. Daniel is an a. only child.
5. Rocky is Daniel's b. dog.
6. Daniel is good at b. soccer.

Exercise 4 – Find the Verb To Be in the Text
Students underline/list all instances. Key forms found in "Meet Daniel":
am — I am 15 years old / I am from Mexico / I am in class 10b / I am an only child / I am usually at home / I am not very good / I am also a fan / I am happy
is — My name is Daniel / My school is big and modern / My family is not very big / He is small and very playful / Life is great!
are — My teachers are friendly and helpful / My parents are teachers / They are very kind and supportive / Their songs are amazing! / my friends are important to me
Exercise 5 – Vocabulary in Context
  • 1. modern → c. new and up-to-date
  • 2. supportive → d. gives help and encouragement
  • 3. friendly → a. easy to talk to / kind (person who is nice and approachable)
  • 4. fan → b. likes something very much
Exercise 6 – Critical Thinking
Which sentence tells you Daniel is friendly / happy?
→ "I am happy and my friends are important to me. Life is great!"
Also: "My teachers are friendly and helpful" — shows he values friendly people, implying he is also friendly.
Exercise 7 – Reflection (open — 1 sentence about yourself)
Accept any correct verb to be sentence about the student. Examples:
I am a student from Colombia. I am 13 years old. I am happy at school.
Teacher Tip
Before reading, activate background knowledge: "Look at the picture — where do you think he is from? How old is he?" For Ex 3B (True/False), require students to write the line from the text that proves their answer. This develops the habit of evidence-based reading at A1 level. Ex 4 (Find the Verb) is great for class scanning practice: set a 3-minute timer and see who finds all instances first.
Common Student Mistakes

✓ Daniel is 15 years old.
✗ He are a student.
✓ He is a student.
✗ His teachers is friendly.
✓ His teachers are friendly.
✗ Rocky are small.
✓ Rocky is small.
Teacher Extra Activity
My "Meet Me" Paragraph (10–15 min)
Students write their own "Meet [Name]" paragraph (4–6 sentences) modeled on Daniel's text. They must use is, am, and are at least once each. Then pairs read each other's paragraph and write 2 True/False statements for their partner to answer. Combines reading, writing, and speaking.
