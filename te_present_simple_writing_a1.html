Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Write Sentences Using the Verbs in Brackets
  • 1. I get up at 7 o'clock every day.
  • 2. My brother plays the guitar.
  • 3. We go to school by bus.
  • 4. She doesn't like spicy food.
  • 5. You have a dog.
  • 6. They watch TV in the evening.
  • 7. He doesn't eat meat.
  • 8. I study English on Mondays.
Exercise 2 – Complete the Email (Lucas to Anna)
Hi Anna,
My name is Lucas and I (1) am 14 years old. I (2) live in a small town near the beach. I (3) go to school from Monday to Friday. My favorite subject (4) is Science.
In my free time, I (5) play football with my friends. We (6) meet at the park on Saturdays. I (7) don't like shopping, but my sister (8) loves it!
Write back soon.
Best, Lucas
Exercise 3 – Write About Your Daily Routine (open)
Personal — accept any grammatically correct present simple sentences. Suggested model:
I usually get up at 7 o'clock. I have breakfast and then I go to school by bus. After school, I do my homework and study English. In the evening, I watch TV with my family. I go to bed at 10 o'clock.
Exercise 4 – Look at the Pictures and Write Sentences
Accept any correct present simple sentence describing the image.
  • 1. She reads a book. / She reads every day.
  • 2. He plays football / soccer.
  • 3. She eats lunch. / She cooks dinner.
  • 4. He listens to music.
  • 5. She sleeps. / She goes to bed.
Exercise 5 – Answer the Questions About You (personal)
Open — accept any correct present simple answer with personal information.
  • 1. I get up at [time] on weekdays.
  • 2. I like [subjects] at school.
  • 3. I [activity] in my free time.
  • 4. Yes, I have a [pet]. His/Her name is [name]. / No, I don't have any pets.
  • 5. I eat [food] for breakfast.
Teacher Tip
The key rule to drill before Ex 1: he/she/it + verb + -s or -es. Write "play → plays / study → studies / go → goes / have → has" on the board. For negatives: don't / doesn't + base verb (never doesn't plays or doesn't goes). For Ex 2 (email), remind students that item 1 uses "be" (am), so the answer is not "have" 14 years old. For Ex 4, encourage frequency adverbs from the Useful Words box: "She usually reads."
Common Student Mistakes
✗ My brother play the guitar.
✓ My brother plays the guitar.
✗ She don't like spicy food.
✓ She doesn't like spicy food.
✗ He doesn't eats meat.
✓ He doesn't eat meat. (base verb after doesn't)
✗ I study English in Mondays.
✓ I study English on Mondays.
Teacher Extra Activity
Write Back to Lucas! (10–15 min)
Students write a reply email to Lucas (4–6 sentences) using the same structure: introduce themselves, say where they live, what they do in free time, and one thing they like/don't like. They swap emails with a partner who underlines any verb form errors. Great for peer correction using the worksheet grammar rules as a reference.
