Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Choose the Correct Demonstrative
Only items 1 and 4 have explicit context (over there / picture of friends in the park). Items 2, 3, 5 default to near forms; accept far forms if justified.
1. That is my sister over there.
2. These are my new shoes.
3. This is a very nice hotel.
4. These are my friends in the park.
5. These are the keys on the table.
Exercise 2 – Complete the Sentences
1. This tree is very old.
2. These books are really interesting.
3. This is my favorite coffee shop.
4. These pens are from my brother.
5. Those mountains are beautiful.
6. This is a small restaurant.
Exercise 3 – Look at the Picture (open, kitchen)
Accept any 5 correct sentences using this/that/these/those. Suggested model:
1. This is a plant. 2. This is a laptop. 3. This is a mug. 4. These are books. 5. This is a desk.
Exercise 4 – Rewrite the Sentences (near / far given)
  • 1. (near) This is my phone.
  • 2. (far) Those are some cows.
  • 3. (near) This is my notebook.
  • 4. (far) That is that big building. (as printed in the worksheet)
  • 5. (near) These are my pencils.
Exercise 5 – True or False (grammar check)
SentenceT/F
1. This is my classroom.T
2. Those are my friends.T
3. That is a good movie.T
4. These are my bicycles.T
5. That are my parents.F
This exercise checks grammatical correctness. Sentence 5 is incorrect: "That" is singular but "are" is plural — it should be "Those are my parents."
Exercise 6 – Match the Sentences
The worksheet has more singular stems (1 "This is", 3 "That is") than singular endings (only "b. a beautiful garden"), and item 5 duplicates item 2's stem ("Those are" appears twice) instead of a different one. A perfect 1:1 match isn't possible as printed. Best-effort pairing below — flag item 3 with the class as an open discussion.
  • 1. This is → b. a beautiful garden.
  • 2. Those are → e. the cats over there.
  • 3. That is → a. my new glasses. (imperfect — no clean singular option remains; worksheet design issue)
  • 4. These are → d. my favorite books.
  • 5. Those are → c. my cousins.
Exercise 7 – Put the Words in Order
  • 1. This is my dog.
  • 2. Those are old houses.
  • 3. That is my teacher.
  • 4. These are my new shoes.
  • 5. Those are very tall trees.
Exercise 8 – Choose and Write
1. (This / That) is my lunch.
2. (These / Those) are my markers.
3. (This / That) is a beautiful day.
4. (These / Those) are our seats.
5. (This / That) is my homework.
Exercise 9 – Mini Dialogues
  • 1. A: What is this? — B: This is my new camera.
  • 2. A: What are these? — B: These are my tickets for the concert.
  • 3. A: What is that over there? — B: That is a famous museum.
Exercise 10 – Write About the Picture (open, 4–5 sentences)
Accept any correct description of the living/dining room picture. Suggested model:
This is a sofa. This is a coffee table. That is a bookshelf in the corner. These are some pillows. That is a lamp near the window.
Teacher Tip
This worksheet works best as a review after teaching the Grammar, Vocabulary, Reading and Writing worksheets. For Ex 5, clarify that "True or False" here means "is the grammar correct?" — not a reading comprehension check. For Ex 6, be upfront that the worksheet has a design issue (more singular stems than singular endings) and use it as a teaching moment about the importance of subject-verb-noun agreement.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ That are my parents.
✓ Those are my parents.
✗ This are my dog.
✓ This is my dog.
✗ Those is old houses.
✓ Those are old houses.
✗ What is these? (mismatched number)
✓ What is this? / What are these?
Teacher Extra Activity
Error Auction (10 min)
Write 8 this/that/these/those sentences on the board — some correct, some with number-agreement errors (like Ex 5, item 5). Groups "bid" points on the sentences they believe are correct, then reveal the answers. Great review activity to close a unit on demonstratives.
