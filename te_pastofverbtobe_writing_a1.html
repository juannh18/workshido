Answer Key
Model Paragraph (Sample Answer)

Yesterday I was at the park with my friends. We were happy and excited because the weather was warm. In the morning, I was at home and I was a little tired. In the afternoon, my friends were at the park too, and we were busy playing football. It was a great day!

Accept any 4–5 sentence paragraph that uses was/were correctly with at least one time expression and one feeling adjective from the Writing Toolbox.

Teaching Support
Teacher Tip
Require students to answer all 5 Plan Your Writing questions before starting the paragraph (Activity 3) — paragraphs planned this way consistently use was/were more accurately than ones written directly from the picture prompts.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ I were at home.
✓ I was at home.
✗ My friends was at the park.
✓ My friends were at the park.
Extra Class Activity
Picture Prompt Swap (10 min)

Students choose one of the three Picture Prompts (Activity 4) they didn't write about and add 2 oral sentences describing it with was/were, sharing with a partner.

Yesterday Was Special • Writing • A1
Lesson Plan (35–40 minutes)
Level: A1 Skill: Writing Topic: Past simple of to be: was/were — guided paragraph Exercises: 6 (1–6), guided writing task Est. Time: 35–40 minutes
Materials & Prep
Printed worksheet copies (1 per student) Pencils or pens
TimeStageProcedure
6 minWarm-UpRead the Writing Toolbox (1) together: was/were rules, time expressions, adjectives and places.
6 minModel SentencesStudents read the 3 model sentences and identify the subject + was/were pattern in each.
8 minPlan Your Writing (2)Students answer 5 planning questions about their own day yesterday.
12 minWrite Your Paragraph (3)Students write 4–5 sentences using their plan and the toolbox vocabulary.
5 minPicture Prompts (4)Students write one caption sentence for at least one of the three pictures.
3 minCheck Your Writing (5–6)Students self-check with the checklist, then answer the Think & Share question.
Follow-Up / Homework Idea

Homework: add 2 more sentences to the paragraph describing how the day ended, using was/were.

For Extra Support

For students who struggle to start, have them answer only the Plan Your Writing questions (2) as short phrases first, then turn each answer into a full sentence with was/were.

For an Extra Challenge

Students write a caption sentence for all three Picture Prompts (4), not just one, using a different feeling adjective each time.

Extension & Fast-Finisher Ideas
Add one more time expression to your paragraph (e.g. last night). Write a negative sentence about something that wasn't true yesterday. Ask a partner: 'Where were you yesterday afternoon?' Circle every was/were in your paragraph and count them.
Key Language & Board Notes
Target: was (I/he/she/it), were (you/we/they) in a personal narrative paragraph.
Board: I was at home yesterday afternoon. We were happy at the park. My parents were tired last night.
Exit Ticket — Quick Check
  • 1. Complete: Yesterday I ___ at home. → was
  • 2. Complete: My friends ___ at the park. → were
  • 3. Write one adjective to describe how you felt yesterday.
