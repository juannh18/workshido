Teaching Support
Teacher Tip
The worksheet's own Teacher Tip box already suggests noticing time expressions — reinforce this by having students underline yesterday/last night/last week in Activities 1 and 3 before answering, since the time expression never changes which form (was/were) is correct, only the subject does.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ She were tired.
✓ She was tired.
✗ Was they at the party?
✓ Were they at the party?
✗ I weren't at home.
✓ I wasn't at home.
Extra Class Activity
Was/Were Musical Chairs (10 min)

Call out a subject pronoun (I, you, he, she, it, we, they); students race to say was or were correctly before you count to 3. Wrong or slow answers sit out one round.

Lesson Plan (35–40 minutes)
Level: A1 Skill: Grammar Topic: Past simple of to be: was/were Exercises: 4 (1–4) + Teacher Tip Est. Time: 35–40 minutes
Materials & Prep
Printed worksheet copies (1 per student) Pencils or pens
TimeStageProcedure
7 minWarm-UpRead the Learn It! table together, then the 4 example sentences, checking was vs. were for each subject.
7 minComplete with WAS or WERE (1)Students complete 8 sentences with the correct form.
6 minWrite the Negative Form (2)Students rewrite 6 affirmative sentences in the negative.
10 minMake Questions and Short Answers (3)Students form 5 yes/no questions and write short answers for both.
7 minChoose or Rewrite (4)Students choose the correct option or rewrite 5 sentences.
3 minWrap-UpReview the Note box: was with I/he/she/it, were with you/we/they.
Follow-Up / Homework Idea

Homework: write 5 sentences about where you and your family were last weekend, using was and were correctly.

For Extra Support

Give a reference card with just the subject pronouns split into two columns (was: I/he/she/it | were: you/we/they) so students can check Activities 1–2 without re-reading the full table each time.

For an Extra Challenge

Students write 3 more yes/no questions with was/were about their own weekend and answer them with true short answers.

Extension & Fast-Finisher Ideas
Name two subjects that use was. Write a negative sentence about yesterday using wasn't. Ask a partner: 'Were you at school yesterday?' Find and fix one more incorrect was/were sentence you can think of.
Key Language & Board Notes
Target: Past simple of to be — was (I/he/she/it), were (you/we/they); negative wasn't/weren't; questions Was/Were...?
Board: I was at home yesterday. They were happy last night. Were you tired after class?
Exit Ticket — Quick Check
  • 1. I ___ (be) at the park yesterday. → was
  • 2. They ___ (be) not late. → weren't
  • 3. ___ she at school? → Was
