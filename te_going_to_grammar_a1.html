Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Complete the Sentences (use am, is or are)
1. I am going to visit my grandparents this weekend.
2. She is going to study medicine.
3. We are going to have dinner at 7 o'clock.
4. They are going to travel to Japan next year.
5. It is going to be sunny tomorrow.
6. He is going to buy a new laptop.
Exercise 2 – Write Negative Sentences (use the words in parentheses)
  • 1. I am not going to watch a movie.
  • 2. She is not going to call her friend.
  • 3. They are not going to play basketball.
  • 4. He is not going to visit London.
  • 5. We are not going to have a picnic.
Exercise 3 – Make Questions (use the words in parentheses)
  • 1. Are you going to study English tomorrow?
  • 2. Is he going to play the guitar?
  • 3. Are they going to visit their aunt?
  • 4. Is she going to cook dinner tonight?
  • 5. Are we going to see a movie?
Exercise 4 – Complete the Questions with Am, Is or Are + Short Answers
QuestionYesNo
1. Are you going to clean your room?Yes, I am.No, I'm not.
2. Is he going to join us later?Yes, he is.No, he isn't.
3. Are they going to finish the project tomorrow?Yes, they are.No, they aren't.
4. Is she going to learn to swim?Yes, she is.No, she isn't.
5. Is it going to rain this afternoon?Yes, it is.No, it isn't.
Exercise 5 – Complete the Sentences (going to + verb from box)
Box: visit • study • have • not watch • go • play • buy
  • 1. I am going to study my English test tomorrow.
  • 2. We are going to buy a new car next month.
  • 3. She is going to visit her friend at the airport.
  • 4. They are going to play soccer this Saturday.
  • 5. He is not going to watch TV tonight. He is tired.
  • 6. It is going to have snow this winter.
  • 7. You are going to go to the gym after school.
Exercise 6 – Write Sentences About Your Plans (suggested answers)
  • 1. I am going to study English this weekend.
  • 2. I am going to visit my family next month.
  • 3. I am not going to watch TV tonight.
  • 4. I am going to learn something new this year.
Accept any grammatically correct going to sentence about the student's own plans.
Teacher Tip
Drill Ex 1 chorally first: say the subject aloud and have students respond with the correct form of be. For Ex 4, emphasize that short answers use the auxiliary verb only — never repeat the full verb. Model: "Are you going to cook? Yes, I am." Then pair students to ask and answer the questions in Ex 4 before writing.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ She going to study medicine.
✓ She is going to study medicine.
✗ He is not going to visited London.
✓ He is not going to visit London.
✗ Are they going to plays basketball?
✓ Are they going to play basketball?
✗ Yes, I am going to. / Yes, I going.
✓ Yes, I am. (short answer only)
Teacher Extra Activity
Going To Drill — Chain Game (5–10 min)
Student 1 says a plan: "I am going to travel to Japan." Student 2 negates it and adds a new plan: "She is not going to travel to Japan. I am going to study English." Continue around the class. Covers affirmative, negative, and agreement of am/is/are in context.
