Answer Key
Before Reading (open answers)
1. What room do you think this is? → A bedroom / a bedroom-office.
2. What things can you see in the room? → A bed, a desk, a chair, a lamp, a bookshelf, a plant (accept any reasonable item).
Vocabulary in Context
1. headboard → D
2. nightstand → A
3. bookshelf → B
4. basket → C
Reading Comprehension
1. Where is the bed? → b) Next to the window.
2. What is on the nightstand? → b) A lamp.
3. True or FalseTrueFalse
The desk is behind the bookshelf.✗✓
The text says "The bookshelf is behind the desk" — so the desk is in FRONT of the bookshelf, not behind it.
4. Where is the plant? → On the shelf, above the bed.
5. What is under the bed? → A basket (with her books in it).
Look Again (open — any six of the following)
next to (the window) · on (the bed) · in front of (the headboard) · next to (the bed) · on (it) · in front of (the bookshelf) · on (the desk) · in front of (it) · behind (the desk) · on (the shelf) · above (the bed) · under (the bed) · in (the basket)
Personal Response (open, suggested answers)
1. My bed is next to the window.
2. My desk is in front of my bookshelf.
Teacher Tip
Before the comprehension questions, have students underline every preposition of place in the text (there are more than six). This primes them for the "Look Again" activity and makes it easier to answer "where" questions accurately.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ The bed is next the window.
✓ The bed is next to the window.
✗ The basket is in front the bed.
✓ The basket is under the bed.
✗ The bookshelf is behind of the desk.
✓ The bookshelf is behind the desk.
✗ The plant is on above the bed.
✓ The plant is on the shelf above the bed.
Teacher Extra Activity
Draw the Room (10–15 min)
In pairs, one student describes the bedroom from the text while the other draws it based only on the description (no looking at the worksheet). Then compare the drawing with the original text and discuss any differences.

Lesson Plan (45 minutes)
TimeStageProcedure
5 minWarm-UpShow the room photo and ask the Before Reading questions orally as a class before students write anything.
10 minPresentationRead the text aloud once while students follow along, then pre-teach headboard, nightstand, bookshelf and basket using the picture.
8 minControlled PracticeStudents complete Vocabulary in Context individually, then check in pairs.
10 minGuided PracticeStudents re-read the text and answer Reading Comprehension questions 1–5, checking their location clues against the text.
8 minProductionStudents complete Look Again, underlining prepositions, then write their Personal Response sentences.
4 minWrap-UpVolunteers share one sentence from Personal Response; correct any preposition errors together on the board.
