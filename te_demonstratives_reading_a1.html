Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Read the Text: "Tom's Room"
No answers needed. Key facts: This = Tom's bed (comfortable) / That = his desk over there, near the window / These = his books on the shelf, his new shoes / Those = his posters on the wall, his old shoes under the bed / This = his plant / That = his sister's plant on the shelf.
Exercise 2 – Reading Comprehension
  • 1. Whose room is it? — It's Tom's room.
  • 2. What is comfortable? — His bed is comfortable.
  • 3. Where is Tom's desk? — It's over there, near the window.
  • 4. What are on the shelf? — His books (and his sister's plant).
  • 5. What are on the wall? — His posters of his favorite bands.
  • 6. Where are Tom's old shoes? — They are under the bed.
  • 7. What does Tom do every day? — He takes care of his plant every day.
  • 8. Whose plant is on the shelf? — It's his sister's plant.
Exercise 3 – Find Words in the Text
1. a place to sleep → bed
2. a table to study → desk
3. pictures on the wall → posters
4. not old → new
5. underneath → under
6. a small green plant → plant
7. take care of → take care of
8. favorite → favorite
Exercise 4 – True or False
StatementTF
1. Tom doesn't like his room.F
2. His desk is near the window.T
3. He has many books on the shelf.T
4. The posters are of movies.F
5. His new shoes are under the bed.F
6. He takes care of the plant every day.T
7. The plant on the shelf is his.F
8. His room is his favorite place.T
Item 4: posters are of his favorite bands, not movies. Item 5: it's his OLD shoes under the bed, not the new ones. Item 7: the plant on the shelf is his sister's, not his.
Exercise 5 – Complete the Sentences
⚠ Most items have no explicit near/far cue. Defaults shown below; accept the alternative if justified. Item 3 has a clear cue ("over there").
1. This is my backpack. It's blue.
2. These are my pens on the table.
3. That is my teacher over there.
4. These are my friends at the park.
5. This is a beautiful day!
6. These are the tickets for the concert.
7. This is your phone ringing.
8. These are my cousins visiting tomorrow.
Exercise 6 – Look at the Picture (open, living room)
Accept any 5 correct sentences describing the picture. Suggested model:
1. This is a sofa. 2. This is a coffee table. 3. That is a bookshelf. 4. These are some books. 5. That is a lamp.
Exercise 7 – Short Answer
  • 1. What is on Tom's desk? — There is a laptop and a lamp on his desk. (based on the picture)
  • 2. What does he like on the wall? — He likes posters of his favorite bands.
  • 3. Where are the old shoes? — They are under the bed.
Teacher Tip
Before reading, have students look at the picture and predict what's in Tom's room. For Ex 4 (True/False), require students to underline the sentence in the text that proves their answer — this is especially useful for items 4, 5 and 7, which are designed to catch careless reading. For Ex 3, model the scanning strategy: read the definition first, then search the text for a matching word.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ That is my books.
✓ These are my books.
✗ Those is my old shoes.
✓ Those are my old shoes.
✗ This are my bed.
✓ This is my bed.
✗ Confusing "these" (near) with "those" (far) when retelling the text.
✓ Re-read the colored words in the text as a model.
Teacher Extra Activity
Draw Tom's Room (10–15 min)
Students draw a simple floor plan of Tom's room based only on the text (bed, desk, shelf with books, posters, shoes under the bed, plant). Pairs compare drawings and check for missing details — reinforces close reading of the demonstrative phrases.
