Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Choose the Correct Option
1. a) there is — a book on the table.
2. b) there are — two windows.
3. b) there are — some plants on the shelf.
4. a) there is — a fridge in the kitchen.
5. b) there are — three chairs around the table.
Exercise 2 – Complete the Sentences
1. There is a lamp on the desk.
2. There are some cups in the cupboard.
3. There is a garden behind the house.
4. There are four bedrooms in the house.
5. There aren't any books on the shelf.
6. There is a TV in the living room.
Item 5 uses "any," which signals the negative form — see Teacher Tip.
Exercise 3 – Look at the Picture (kitchen, open)
Accept any 5 correct sentences describing the kitchen picture. Suggested model:
1. There is a kitchen table. 2. There are four chairs around the table. 3. There are some cabinets in the kitchen. 4. There is a fridge near the window. 5. There are some windows in the kitchen.
Exercise 4 – Make Negative Sentences
  • 1. There isn't a mirror in the bathroom.
  • 2. There aren't any pillows on the bed.
  • 3. There isn't a plant on the desk.
  • 4. There aren't any pictures on the wall.
  • 5. There isn't a carpet on the floor.
Exercise 5 – Answer the Questions (short answers)
  • 1. Is there a table in the kitchen? — Yes, there is.
  • 2. Are there any windows in the bedroom? — No, there aren't.
  • 3. Is there a sofa in the living room? — Yes, there is.
  • 4. Are there some books on the shelf? — Yes, there are.
  • 5. Is there a shower in the bathroom? — No, there isn't.
Exercise 6 – Look and Write (3 pictures, open)
Accept any correct sentences. Suggested models (2 sentences each):
  • 1. Bedroom — There is a bed in the bedroom. There is a plant next to the bed.
  • 2. Bookshelf — There are many books on the shelf. There is a plant on the shelf.
  • 3. Bathroom — There is a sink in the bathroom. There is a shower next to the sink.
Exercise 7 – Read and Complete
This is my house. In the living room, there is a big sofa and a coffee table. There are two windows and a TV on the wall. In the kitchen, there is a table and four chairs. There are some dishes in the cupboard. In my bedroom, there is a bed, a desk and a lamp. There aren't any carpets in the rooms.
Exercise 8 – True or False (grammar check)
SentenceT/F
1. There is a garden in front of the house.T
2. There are three bedrooms in the house.T
3. There is a car in the garage.T
4. There are any TVs in the bedrooms.F
5. There is a bathtub in the bathroom.T
This exercise checks grammatical correctness, not a specific story. Sentences 1, 2, 3 and 5 use is/are correctly. Sentence 4 is incorrect — "any" cannot be used in an affirmative statement; it should be "There aren't any TVs in the bedrooms" or "Are there any TVs in the bedrooms?"
Exercise 9 – Choose and Write (word box, open)
Open — students choose 5 words from the box (sofa, plant, fridge, mirror, bed, shelves, carpet, lamp, window, desk) and write correct sentences. Suggested model:
1. There is a sofa in the living room. 2. There is a plant on the desk. 3. There is a fridge in the kitchen. 4. There is a mirror in the bathroom. 5. There is a bed in the bedroom.
Exercise 10 – Write About Your Room (personal, 5–6 sentences)
Open — accept any correct paragraph. Suggested model:
There is a bed in my room. There is a desk next to the window. There are two shelves with books. There is a lamp on the desk. There are some pictures on the wall. There is a carpet on the floor.
Teacher Tip
This worksheet works best as a review/consolidation after the Grammar, Vocabulary, Reading and Writing worksheets. For Ex 8, clarify that "True or False" here means "Is the grammar of the sentence correct?" — not a reading comprehension check. Use sentence 4 as a live example on the board: "Why is 'any' wrong here?"
Common Student Mistakes
✗ There is three chairs.
✓ There are three chairs.
✗ There not a mirror.
✓ There isn't a mirror.
✗ There are any TVs.
✓ There aren't any TVs. / Are there any TVs?
✗ Yes, there is a sofa. (long form)
✓ Yes, there is. (short answer)
Teacher Extra Activity
Error Auction (10 min)
Write 8 there is/there are sentences on the board — some correct, some with errors (singular/plural mismatch, wrong use of "any"). Groups "bid" points on the sentences they believe are correct, then reveal the answers. Great review activity to close a unit on there is/there are.

Lesson Plan (50 minutes)
TimeStageProcedure
5 minWarm-UpQuick oral review: teacher says a noun (e.g., "two lamps"), students respond with the full sentence ("There are two lamps.").
10 minControlled PracticeStudents complete Ex 1–2 individually, then check in pairs. Class correction with focus on item 5 of Ex 2 ("any" → negative).
10 minGuided PracticeStudents complete Ex 3–4–5 in pairs, alternating who writes and who checks.
10 minReading & Grammar FocusStudents complete Ex 6–7 individually. As a class, complete Ex 8 together on the board, discussing why sentence 4 is incorrect.
12 minProductionStudents complete Ex 9 (choosing their own words) and start Ex 10 (personal paragraph about their room).
3 minWrap-UpVolunteers share one sentence from Ex 10. Assign any unfinished exercises as homework.
