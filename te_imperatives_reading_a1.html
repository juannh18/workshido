Exercise 1 – Before You Read
Picture: "Welcome to Green Park" sign → b. Park rules
Exercise 2 – Reading: "Rules for Everyone" (Text 2)
Sign text: Use the trash bins, keep the park clean / Ride your bike on the paths, be safe / Keep your dog on a leash, respect others / Be quiet, enjoy the nature / Don't pick the flowers, protect the plants.
  • 1. What should you use in the park? — The trash bins.
  • 2. Where should you ride your bike? — On the paths.
  • 3. What should you keep your dog on? — A leash.
  • 4. What should you not do in the park? — Pick the flowers.
  • 5. Why should people be quiet? — To enjoy the nature / to respect others.
Exercise 3 – Read for Details: "How to Take Care of Your Tablet" (Text 3)
  • 1. You should keep your tablet clean.
  • 2. You shouldn't eat or drink near it.
  • 3. You should use a soft cloth to clean the screen.
  • 4. You shouldn't drop your tablet.
  • 5. You should charge it with the original charger.
  • 6. You should turn off the tablet when you are not using it.

  • 4. maintain in a good condition (text 1) → keep clean / keep our park clean and safe
  • 5. device used to give power (text 3) → charger

Exercise 7 – Personal Response (open, 3 sentences)
Accept any 3 reasonable classroom rules. Suggested model:
Raise your hand before speaking. Keep your desk clean. Listen to the teacher.
Think! box (open discussion): Why are imperatives important in signs, rules and instructions? — Suggested answer: Because they give clear, short and direct information so people know exactly what to do quickly, which matters for safety and understanding.
Teacher Tip
Before reading, ask students to predict the topic from the sign picture alone (Ex 1) — this builds prediction skills. For Ex 4, require students to point to the exact line in the text that proves each answer. For Ex 6, openly acknowledge the garbled item so students don't lose confidence thinking they misunderstood the language.

Teacher Extra Activity
Design a Sign (10–15 min)
In pairs, students design their own "Rules for Everyone" sign for a place of their choice (school, bus, swimming pool) using at least 4 imperative rules, following the Text 2 format (rule + short benefit phrase).
