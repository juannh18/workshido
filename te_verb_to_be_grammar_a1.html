Teacher Tip
For Ex 1, do the chart row by row chorally — say the subject and students respond with the complete sentence. Drill contractions separately (isn't / aren't). In Ex 2, have students read the full corrected sentence aloud after circling, not just the word — this builds fluency. For Ex 3, collect one sentence per student on the board and correct collaboratively.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ She are my sister.
✓ She is my sister.
✗ I is from Canada.
✓ I am from Canada.
✗ They is at school.
✓ They are at school.
✗ He not tired. / She not happy.
✓ He is not tired. / She is not happy.
Teacher Extra Activity
True / False Quiz (5 min)
Read sentences aloud: "Michael Jordan is from Canada." / "Penguins are birds." / "The Amazon is a country." Students respond "True" or "False" and correct false statements using the verb to be. Quick, high-energy, zero prep.
