Answer Key
B. Sentence Builder
  • 1. My friend's backpack is black.
  • 2. The baby's bottle is on the bed.
  • 3. The teacher's desk is near the window.
  • 4. My parents' car is very fast.
  • 5. The boys' room is messy.
  • 6. Emma and Jack's dog is very playful.
Watch items 4 and 5: parents and boys are plurals ending in -s, so only the apostrophe is added (parents', boys'). Item 6 is joint possession: the 's goes only on the last name (Emma and Jack's), not on both.
C. Guided Writing – Suggested Sentences (open)
  • 1. This is my brother's bedroom.
  • 2. My brother's desk is near the window.
  • 3. My brother's backpack is on the floor.
  • 4. My brother's ball is next to the bed.
  • 5. My brother's bed is very comfortable.
Open answers — the picture shows a bedroom with a desk, a lamp, a chair, a backpack, a soccer ball and a bed. Accept any owner the student invents (my sister's, Tom's...) as long as the possessive 's is correct and the object appears in the picture.
D. Your Turn – Model Paragraph (open)
  • My name is Daniel and this is my family. My mother's name is Rosa. My father's car is blue and very old. My sister's room is next to my room. My grandparents' house is in the city. My best friend's name is Leo. Leo's dog plays with us every weekend. I love my family's Sunday lunch.
Require 6–8 sentences and at least four different possessive forms. Use the checklist in E as the marking rubric.
E. Writing Checklist
  • No fixed answers — students self-check their own text: possessive 's used correctly, complete sentences, capital letters and punctuation, clear organization.
Teacher Tip
Before Section D, have students write a quick "ownership map": their name in the middle and five people around it, each with one object (mom → kitchen, Leo → dog). Writing from a map removes the "I don't know what to write" block and every branch becomes one possessive sentence.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ My parent's car is very fast. (two parents)
✓ My parents' car is very fast.
✗ Emma's and Jack's dog is playful. (one shared dog)
✓ Emma and Jack's dog is playful.
✗ The backpack of my friend is black.
✓ My friend's backpack is black.
✗ My brothers bedroom is big.
✓ My brother's bedroom is big.
Teacher Extra Activity
Picture Dictation in Reverse (10 min)
One student secretly chooses an object in the classroom and says only whose it is: "It's Sofia's..." The class asks yes/no questions to guess the object ("Is Sofia's object blue?"). Every question and answer forces a new possessive phrase.

Materials & Prep

Lesson Plan (45–50 minutes)
TimeStageProcedure
5 minWarm-UpRead the four Useful Language sentences (A) aloud. Students underline every 's and ' and say who the owner is in each sentence.
10 minControlled PracticeStudents complete Sentence Builder (B) individually. Check on the board, spelling out the apostrophe position for items 4, 5 and 6 (parents', boys', Emma and Jack's).
10 minGuided WritingLook at the picture in C together and elicit the visible objects (desk, lamp, backpack, ball, bed). Students write their five sentences; monitor and correct apostrophes on the spot.
5 minPre-WritingStudents draw their ownership map (see Teacher Tip): five people + one object each. Quick pair-share of maps before writing.
15 minProductionStudents write their 6–8 sentence paragraph in D using their map, then self-check with the checklist (E) and tick each box honestly. Volunteers read their paragraph aloud.
5 minWrap-UpPeer review: partners exchange texts and circle every possessive. The pair with the most correct possessives reads one sentence to the class.
Follow-Up / Homework Idea
Students take (or draw) a picture of one room at home and write 5 sentences about what belongs to whom in that room (my dad's chair, my sister's books...). Next class, they show the picture while the class listens and counts the possessives.
For Extra Support For Section D, give a sentence-starter frame on the board: My ______'s ______ is ______. Weaker students complete the frame six times before writing freely. Reduce the target to 5 sentences.
For an Extra Challenge Ask fast finishers to include one plural owner (my parents' ...) and one joint possession (... and ...'s) in their paragraph, and to label each type in the margin.
