Teaching Support
Teacher Tip
Pre-teach all four Vocabulary in Context words (arrived, guide, colorful, experiment) before reading — each one appears verbatim in the passage, so students can confirm meaning by finding it in context as they read.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ The museum were old.
✓ The museum was old.
✗ The dinosaur bones was big.
✓ The dinosaur bones were big.
Extra Class Activity
Museum Memory Race (10 min)

In teams, students have 60 seconds to write as many true was/were sentences as they can remember about the reading (e.g. 'The rooms were big'). The team with the most correct sentences wins.

A Day at the Museum • Reading • A1
Lesson Plan (35–40 minutes)
Level: A1 Skill: Reading Topic: Past simple of to be: was/were Exercises: 3 (A–C) + Think & Share Est. Time: 35–40 minutes
Materials & Prep
Printed worksheet copies (1 per student) Pencils or pens
TimeStageProcedure
5 minBefore You ReadStudents discuss the two warm-up questions in pairs about their own museum experience.
10 minReadingStudents read A Day at the Museum silently, then check the 4 Vocabulary in Context words against the passage.
8 minTrue or False (A)Students mark T/F for 5 statements and correct the false ones.
7 minAnswer the Questions (B)Students answer 5 comprehension questions in complete sentences.
6 minComplete the Sentences (C)Students fill 5 blanks with was/were using information from the text.
4 minThink & ShareStudents discuss their favorite part of the story in pairs.
Follow-Up / Homework Idea

Homework: write 4 sentences about a place you visited, using was and were.

For Extra Support

Let students underline every was/were in the passage before Activity A — this builds a visual map of subject-verb pairs they can reference for the fill-in-the-blank activity (C).

For an Extra Challenge

Students write 2 extra true/false sentences about the reading for a partner to solve.

Extension & Fast-Finisher Ideas
Name two things that were big in the museum. Write one sentence about how the students felt at the end. Ask a partner what their favorite museum exhibit is. Find every time expression in the text (yesterday, at 9 o'clock, at 3 o'clock).
Key Language & Board Notes
Target: was (I/he/she/it), were (you/we/they/plural nouns) in descriptive past narrative.
Board: The building was old. The rooms were big. We were happy and a little tired.
Exit Ticket — Quick Check
  • 1. What time did the class arrive? → 9 o'clock
  • 2. Were the coins big or small? → small but shiny
  • 3. How did the students feel at the end? → happy and a little tired
