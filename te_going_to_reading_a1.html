Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Pre-Reading
A. Look at the picture and title (open answer)
Accept any reasonable prediction. Example: "The text is about a girl's vacation plans / travel plans for the summer."
B. Who is the text about? / What is it mainly about?
1. Who? c. A traveler (Laura, talking about her summer plans)
2. What? c. Future plans (she describes everything with going to)
Vocabulary Preview — Match
1. trip → short journey
2. visit → to go to see a person or place
3. explore → to discover new places
4. beach → a place near the sea
5. next → the following / upcoming
6. plan → an idea for the future
Exercise 2 – Reading Text
No answer key required — students read the text "My Plans for the Summer" silently. Text is about Laura's summer: visiting grandparents near the beach → trip with Anna to the mountains → online photography course.
Exercise 3 – Comprehension
A. Answer the questions
  • 1. The text is about Laura.
  • 2. Her grandparents live near the beach / in another city.
  • 3. She is going to stay for one week.
  • 4. She is going to travel with Anna, her best friend.
  • 5. They are going to hike and take lots of photos.
  • 6. She is going to take an online course in photography.
B. True or False
StatementTF
1. Laura is going to visit her grandparents at the beach.✓
2. Laura is going to travel alone.✗
3. They are going to explore a town in the mountains.✓
4. Laura is going to take a cooking course.✗
5. Laura is excited about her summer.✓
Exercise 4 – Vocabulary in Context
  • 1. "take a trip" → a. a short journey
  • 2. "going to explore a beautiful town" → a. to discover new places
  • 3. "My grandparents live near the beach" → b. the sea and sand
Exercise 5 – Inference (use information from the text)
1. Because her grandparents live near the beach and she loves the beach / the sea.
2. She likes swimming, sunbathing, eating ice cream, hiking, and taking photos.
3. She feels excited and happy — she thinks it's going to be an unforgettable summer.
Exercise 6 – Reflection (open answers — about the student)
Accept any grammatically correct going to response. Encourage full sentences.
What are you going to do this summer? → I am going to [visit / travel / study / rest]...
Where are you going to go? → I am going to go to [place].
Who are you going to go with? → I am going to go with [family / friends].
What are you most excited about? → I am most excited about [activity].
Teacher Tip
Before reading, do the Vocabulary Preview in Ex 1 as a class — it pre-teaches all the key words students need to understand the text. For Ex 3B (True/False), ask students to underline the exact sentence in the text that justifies each answer. This builds the habit of evidence-based comprehension at A1 level.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ She going to visit her grandparents.
✓ She is going to visit her grandparents.
✗ Laura is going to learns photography.
✓ Laura is going to learn photography.
✗ They go to hike in the mountains.
✓ They are going to hike in the mountains.
✗ "explore" = to build a town (distractor c)
✓ "explore" = to discover new places
Teacher Extra Activity
My Summer Plans Paragraph (10–15 min)
Students write a short paragraph (4–6 sentences) about their own summer plans, using Laura's text as a model. They must include at least one affirmative, one negative, and one question using going to. Pairs then share their plans and ask one follow-up question.
