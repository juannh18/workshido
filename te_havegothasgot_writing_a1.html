Answer Key
1. Sentence Builder
  • 1. I have got a blue school bag. (I've got a blue school bag.)
  • 2. My sister has got long hair.
  • 3. We have got a small garden.
  • 4. He has got a new football.
  • 5. They have got two cats.
  • 6. I haven't got a laptop.
  • 7. She hasn't got a bicycle.
  • 8. My friend has got a music lesson today.
2. Describe the Picture (open, suggested answers)
1. My brother has got a desk in his bedroom.
2. He has got a teddy bear on the shelf.
3. He has got a lot of books.
4. He has got a bike.
Word bank given on the worksheet: mother, father, brother, bedroom, desk, books, teddy bear, bike. Suggested answers use only these words — accept any other sentence that uses "have got / has got" correctly with them.
3. Ask and Write (open — question form is fixed, answers are personal)
1. Q: Have you got a pet? A: Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
2. Q: Has your best friend got a bike? A: Yes, he/she has. / No, he/she hasn't.
3. Q: Has your classroom got a computer? A: Yes, it has. / No, it hasn't.
4. Q: Has your family got a car? A: Yes, it has. / No, it hasn't.
4. My Family and Our Things & 5. Writing Checklist (open)
No fixed answer key — personal paragraph using the Starter Bank. Grade with the Writing Checklist: correct have got/has got, capital letters and full stops, clear sentences, correct spelling.
Teacher Tip
Before Exercise 4, have students underline the subject in each Starter Bank sentence ("My family", "I", "My mother"...) and say out loud whether it needs "have got" or "has got" — this prevents the most common error in the free paragraph.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ My sister have got long hair.
✓ My sister has got long hair.
✗ i have got a blue school bag
✓ I have got a blue school bag. (capital letter + full stop)
✗ Has your family a car?
✓ Has your family got a car? (don't drop "got")
✗ I not have got a laptop.
✓ I haven't got a laptop.
Teacher Extra Activity
Guess the Room (10–15 min)
Students swap their Exercise 2 sentences with a partner (without showing the picture). The partner draws the room based only on the sentences, then compares the drawing with the original picture on the worksheet.

Lesson Plan (45 minutes)
TimeStageProcedure

8 minPresentationModel one Sentence Builder item on the board, showing both the affirmative and a negative version.
8 minControlled PracticeStudents complete Sentence Builder individually, then compare with a partner.
10 minGuided PracticeStudents complete Describe the Picture using the bedroom photo and word bank.
10 minProductionStudents complete Ask and Write, then interview a partner for real using those questions.
4 minWrap-UpStudents draft My Family and Our Things and self-check with the Writing Checklist before handing in.
