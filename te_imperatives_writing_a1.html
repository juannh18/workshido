Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Sentence Builder
  • 1. Close the window, please.
  • 2. Don't use your phone in class.
  • 3. Be quiet now.
  • 4. Wash your hands before eating.
  • 5. Take your umbrella, it's raining.
Exercise 2 – Picture Prompts (open, 5 pictures)
Accept any correct imperative per picture. Suggested models:
1. Throw away your trash. 2. Read your book. 3. Turn off the light. 4. Be quiet. 5. Pack your backpack.
Exercise 3 – Guided Writing (word from box)
Box: not forget · turn off · drink · bring · be · put
Two items don't have a clean fit with the remaining box words: item 3 ("drink your own water bottle") is awkward phrasing, and item 4 ("Be late for school") uses the only word left ("be") but contradicts the worksheet's own "good instructions" — it likely intended "Don't be late for school." Flagged below rather than invented as a clean match.
  • 1. Don't forget your homework every day.
  • 2. Turn off the lights when you leave the room.
  • 3. Drink your own water bottle. (awkward as printed — likely meant "Bring your own water bottle")
  • 4. Be late for school. (contradicts good advice — likely meant "Don't be late for school," but box has no "not be" option)
  • 5. Bring your books to class.
  • 6. Put your trash in the bin.
Exercise 4 – Turn into Imperatives
  • 1. You should study every day. → Study every day.
  • 2. Please don't run in the hallway. → Don't run in the hallway.
  • 3. You must clean your desk. → Clean your desk.
  • 4. Don't eat in class. → Don't eat in class. (already imperative)
  • 5. You should listen to the teacher. → Listen to the teacher.
  • 6. Please be quiet. → Be quiet.
Exercise 5 – Write Your Own Instructions (open, 2 per situation)
Accept any 2 correct imperatives per situation. Suggested models:
Using a computer in class: a. Don't visit social media. b. Use the computer only for schoolwork.
Working in a group: a. Listen to your teammates. b. Share your ideas.
Preparing your school bag: a. Pack your books the night before. b. Don't forget your homework.
Exercise 6 – Check Your Writing
No fixed answer key — this is a self-assessment checklist. Students review their own sentences from Ex 1–5 and check Yes/No for: short and clear / base form of the verb / polite and positive / easy to understand / correct spelling.
Teacher Tip
For Ex 3, use item 4 as a teaching moment: ask students why "Be late for school" sounds wrong as advice, then have them practice the negative form "Don't be late for school." For Ex 6, model the self-check process with one of your own example sentences before students check their own work.

✓ Wash your hands before eating.
✗ You study every day. (rewriting task, kept subject)
✓ Study every day. (drop the subject)
✗ Don't forgets your homework.
✓ Don't forget your homework.
Teacher Extra Activity
Instruction Manual (10–15 min)
In pairs, students write a mini "instruction manual" (5–6 imperatives) for a simple everyday task — making a sandwich, brushing teeth, or getting ready for school. Pairs swap manuals and try to follow the steps exactly as written, checking for clarity.
