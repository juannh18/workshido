Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Write Sentences About You (use am, is or are)
Open — personal information. Accept any correct verb to be sentence. Suggested model:
I: I am Maria.
My age: I am 14 years old.
My country: I am from Colombia.
My job (or school): I am a student at Lincoln School.
My friends: My friends are funny and kind.
How I feel today: I am happy today.
Exercise 2 – Unscramble the Words (put in correct order)
  • 1. I am a student.
  • 2. She is from Brazil.
  • 3. They are my friends.
  • 4. He is a teacher.
  • 5. We are happy today.
Exercise 3 – Complete the Paragraph (about Liam)
Hi! My name is Liam. I am 13 years old. I am from Canada. I am a student. My family is small. I am happy to be here.
Exercise 4 – Write Questions (use am, is or are)
  • 1. Are you a student?
  • 2. Is she from Spain?
  • 3. Are they your friends?
  • 4. Is he a teacher?
  • 5. Are we late?
Exercise 5 – Write Short Answers (to questions in Ex 4)
1. Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
2. Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
3. Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
4. Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
5. Yes, we are. / No, we aren't.
Exercise 6 – Describe the Picture (4–5 sentences)
Open — students write about the family picture. Accept any correct verb to be sentences.
"They are a family. There are four people. The mother is tall. The children are young. They are happy together."
Exercise 7 – Write About Your Best Friend (4–5 sentences)
Open — personal description. Accept any correct verb to be sentences.
"My best friend is Ana. She is 14 years old. She is from Mexico. She is very funny and kind. We are in the same class."
Teacher Tip
For Ex 2 (Unscramble), remind students to check that the verb agrees with the subject before writing the sentence — this forces them to identify the subject first. For Ex 6 and 7 (open writing), set a minimum of 4 sentences and require at least one negative sentence (e.g., "She is not from Colombia.") to push beyond simple affirmatives.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ My name Liam. / I Liam.
✓ My name is Liam. / I am Liam.
✗ She are from Brazil.
✓ She is from Brazil.
✗ Are you a student? — Yes, I am a student.
✓ Yes, I am. (short answer — don't repeat the full verb)
✗ I happy today. / They friend.
✓ I am happy today. / They are friends.
Teacher Extra Activity
My Profile Card (10–15 min)
Students create a mini "profile card" with 5 sentences using the verb to be: their name, age, country, school, and how they feel. Then they exchange cards with a partner who writes 2 questions using Is/Are. Partner answers with short answers. Cards can be displayed on the wall.
