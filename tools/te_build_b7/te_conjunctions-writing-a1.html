Answer Key
1. Complete and Write

Students choose a conjunction from the box and finish each sentence with their own idea; several gaps allow more than one conjunction. Best fit: 1 and, 2 but / so, 3 because, 4 so, 5 or, 6 before, 7 and / after, 8 because / when. Check the two halves make sense together.

2. Look and Write

Personal answers – one complete sentence per picture using the conjunction in brackets: 1 (because) e.g. He is studying because he has a test. 2 (and) She is eating a sandwich and drinking juice. 3 (after) They went for a walk after school. 4 (so) The dog was hungry, so it ate.

3. Join the Ideas
  • 1. I was hungry, so we made a sandwich.
  • 2. She didn't want to go because it was cold outside.
  • 3. We watched a movie and ate popcorn.
  • 4. After I got home, I did my homework. (also: I did my homework after I got home.)
  • 5. Do you like tea or coffee?
  • 6. He missed the bus, so he was late.
4. Put the Words in Order
  • 1. I brush my teeth after breakfast.
  • 2. It was raining, but we went for a walk.
  • 3. We went home when it was dark.
  • 4. She likes cats, but she prefers dogs.
  • 5. I study because I want good grades.
  • 6. We played games and had dinner.
5. Complete the Paragraph
1. because
5. and
2. before
6. but
3. so
7. before / and
4. but
8. After

Conjunctions may repeat. Fixed: 1 because, 3 so, 4 & 6 but, 8 After. Gaps 5 and 7 accept any conjunction that fits the sense.

6. Write about You

Personal answers (4–5 sentences about your day, weekend or a favourite activity, at least 3 conjunctions). Use the checklist: 3+ conjunctions, complete sentences, own ideas.

Teaching Support
Teacher Tip
The box gives eight conjunctions but students only need to ask two questions: what is the relationship (adding / contrast / reason / result / choice / time)? and does a comma go before it (usually before 'but' and 'so')? Put those two questions on the board.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ I was hungry so, we made a sandwich.
✓ I was hungry, so we made a sandwich.
✗ She didn't go, it was cold.
✓ She didn't go because it was cold.
✗ Do you like tea and coffee? (choice)
✓ Do you like tea or coffee?
✗ After I got home I did my homework.
✓ After I got home, I did my homework.
Extra Class Activity
Sentence Builder Dice (8 min)
In groups, students have two piles of sentence halves and a list of conjunctions. They draw two halves, pick a conjunction that joins them logically, and write the full sentence with correct punctuation. One point per correct, sensible sentence.

Conjunctions – Writing • A1
Learning Focus
Objective: Students will be able to join two short ideas into one written sentence using and, but, because, so, or and time conjunctions, with correct commas, and produce a short connected paragraph.
Success Criteria: I can choose the right conjunction for the meaning (adding, contrast, reason, result, choice, time). I can join two sentences into one. I can put a comma before 'but' and 'so', and after a fronted time / condition clause.
Prior Knowledge: The meaning of and, but, because, so, or and before / after / when (from the practice and reading worksheets); simple present and past sentences.
Lesson Plan (40–45 minutes)
Level: A1 Skill: Writing Topic: Joining ideas in writing with and, but, because, so, or, when, before, after Exercises: 6 (1–6) Est. Time: 40–45 minutes
Materials & Prep
Printed worksheet copies (1 per student) Pens or pencils Board for the 8 conjunctions grouped by function
TimeStageProcedure
5 minWarm-UpWrite two sentences: 'I was tired.' 'I finished my homework.' Ask students to join them two different ways ('… but I finished…' / '… so I stopped…'). Show how the conjunction changes the meaning.
12 minPresentation (I Do)Group the eight conjunctions on the board by job: add = and; contrast = but; reason = because; result = so; choice = or; time = when, before, after. One model each, joining two clauses. Punctuation: a comma before 'but' and 'so' ('It was late, so we went home.'); a comma after a fronted time / 'if' clause ('After I got home, I rested.'). Model Exercise 3 item 1: 'I was hungry.' + 'We made a sandwich.' + (so) → 'I was hungry, so we made a sandwich.' Point out that 'so' puts the cause first and the result second.
12 minGuided Practice (We Do)Exercise 3 items 1–3 together: label the relationship, choose the conjunction, add the comma, check the sense. Do Exercise 4 item 1 as a class.
10 minIndependent Practice (You Do)Students complete Exercise 3, Exercise 4 (order the words) and Exercise 5 (paragraph) alone; compare Exercise 4 in pairs, checking commas.
6 minAssessmentReview Exercise 4 on the board: students read the sentence, name the conjunction and its job, and say if a comma is needed. Students complete the Exit Ticket individually.
5 minClosureStudents write the first two sentences of Exercise 6 and read them to a partner, who counts the conjunctions and checks the commas.
Follow-Up / Homework Idea
Homework: finish the Exercise 6 paragraph (4–5 sentences, 3+ conjunctions), then underline each conjunction and label its job.
Anticipated Problems & Solutions
Problem: Students put the comma after the conjunction ('I was tired so, I stopped') instead of before it.
Solution: Show the rule on the board with a big comma before 'so' / 'but'; have students point to where the comma goes before writing.
Problem: Students use 'so' and 'because' the wrong way round ('It was cold, because I stayed home').
Solution: Teach the order: cause + so + result; result + because + cause. Ask 'what happened first?' before they write.
For Extra Support Give weaker students Exercises 3 and 5 with the relationship labelled (reason / result / time…) so they only choose the matching conjunction.
For an Extra Challenge Students rewrite three Exercise 4 sentences, changing the conjunction to a different one that still works and adjusting the order and punctuation.
Extension & Fast-Finisher Ideas
Write one sentence that uses two conjunctions. Change 'because' to 'so' in one sentence and reorder it.
Language Focus & Board Notes
Grammar: and / but / because / so / or + time conjunctions when / before / after; comma before 'but' and 'so' and after a fronted clause.
Board: I was hungry, so we made a sandwich. • She likes cats, but she prefers dogs. • After I got home, I did my homework.
Exit Ticket — Quick Check
  • 1. Join: 'It was raining.' + 'We went for a walk.' (but) → It was raining, but we went for a walk.
  • 2. Choose: I study ____ I want good grades. → because
  • 3. Add the comma: After I got home I did my homework. → After I got home, I did my homework.
