Answer Key
1. Read the Text

Text: Alex's Saturday. Students read and circle the conjunctions (and, so, before, because, after, when).

2. Answer the Questions
  • 1. They left home at 7 a.m.
  • 2. They went to a lake in the mountains.
  • 3. They ate a quick breakfast.
  • 4. Because he wanted to see the lake.
  • 5. They had lunch near the water and relaxed.
  • 6. He felt happy and very tired.
3. True or False
1. F (with his family)
4. T
2. T
5. F (he kept going)
3. F (sunny and warm)
6. T
4. Choose the Correct Word
1. a) and
5. a) because
2. a) and
6. b) After
3. b) before
7. a) and
4. b) but
8. a) and
5. Find the Conjunctions (by group)
  • Adding ideas (and): and (got up early and left home; trees and a small waterfall; lunch… and relaxed; happy and very tired)
  • Giving a reason (because): because (I wanted to see the lake)
  • Showing contrast (but): but (I was a little tired, but I kept going)
  • Showing result (so): so (sunny and warm, so we went hiking)
  • Time (when, before, after): before (the hike), after (the hike), when (we got home)
6. Think About It

Personal answers – favourite outdoor activity + why; a trip they would like and what they would do. Encourage a 'because' and a time conjunction in the answer.

Teaching Support
Teacher Tip
This text is short but packed with every target conjunction. Before Exercise 4, have students underline each conjunction in the text and label it (time / reason / result / adding) – the multiple-choice answers then confirm what they already found.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ Alex kept going, he was tired.
✓ Alex kept going, but he was tired.
✗ It was sunny, we went hiking.
✓ It was sunny, so we went hiking.
✗ He was happy when he got home but very tired.
✓ When he got home, he was happy and very tired.
✗ We ate breakfast after the hike.
✓ We ate breakfast before the hike.
Extra Class Activity
Retell with Conjunctions (8 min)
In pairs, students retell Alex's Saturday in 5–6 sentences, using at least four conjunctions from the text. The listener ticks each conjunction they hear. Swap roles.

Conjunctions – Reading • A1
Learning Focus
Objective: Students will be able to read a short narrative, identify the conjunctions and their function, and answer comprehension questions that depend on those conjunctions.
Success Criteria: I can find conjunctions in a text. I can say if a conjunction adds an idea, gives a reason, shows a result or shows time. I can answer 'why' and 'when' questions using because and time conjunctions.
Prior Knowledge: The meaning of and, but, because, so and the time conjunctions before / after / when (from the practice worksheet).
Lesson Plan (40–45 minutes)
Level: A1 Skill: Reading Topic: Conjunctions (and, but, because, so, when, before, after) in a short text Exercises: 6 (1–6) Est. Time: 40–45 minutes
Materials & Prep
Printed worksheet copies (1 per student) Coloured pencils for underlining Pens
TimeStageProcedure
5 minWarm-UpWrite 'I was tired ___ I kept going.' on the board and ask students to fill the gap (but). Then 'It was sunny ___ we went hiking.' (so). Say: today's text uses lots of these small joining words – they carry the meaning.
10 minPresentation (I Do)Read the text aloud. Stop at each conjunction and think aloud about its job: 'so we went hiking' – this is a result (sunny weather → hiking); 'because I wanted to see the lake' – this is a reason (it answers why); 'When we got home' – this is a time. Show how question 4 ('Why did Alex keep going?') is answered by finding 'because' in the text: 'Because he wanted to see the lake.' Point out that 'why' questions are answered with 'because'.
10 minGuided Practice (We Do)Students underline all the conjunctions in the text and label each (add / reason / result / time). Do Exercise 2 questions 1–3 together, locating the sentence first. Do Exercise 4 items 1–2 as a class.
10 minIndependent Practice (You Do)Students complete Exercise 3 (true/false with proof), Exercise 4 (choose the word) and Exercise 5 (sort the conjunctions by group) alone.
6 minAssessmentReview Exercise 5 as a class: students read each conjunction and its group and the phrase it comes from. Students complete the Exit Ticket individually.
5 minClosureStudents answer one question in Exercise 6 in 2–3 sentences and read it to a partner, who names any conjunction used.
Follow-Up / Homework Idea
Homework: write your own short 'My Saturday' text (5–6 sentences) using at least one 'because', one 'so' and two time conjunctions.
Anticipated Problems & Solutions
Problem: Students answer 'why' questions without 'because' ('He wanted to see the lake' with no linker) or use 'so' instead.
Solution: Model: a 'why' question is answered with 'Because…'. Require 'Because' at the start of the Exercise 2 question 4 answer.
Problem: Students mix up 'so' (result) and 'because' (reason) in Exercise 4.
Solution: Test on the board: 'reason comes before, result comes after'. 'It was sunny, so we hiked' (result after) vs 'We hiked because it was sunny' (reason after).
For Extra Support Give weaker students the text with the conjunctions already in bold, so they focus on the function rather than finding them.
For an Extra Challenge Students rewrite two sentences from the text, replacing the conjunction with another one that still makes sense (e.g. 'so' → 'because', reordering as needed).
Extension & Fast-Finisher Ideas
Find the word 'during' in the text – is it a conjunction? (No, it is followed by a noun, not a clause.) Write one more true/false sentence about the text for a partner.
Language Focus & Board Notes
Grammar: and (add), but (contrast), so (result), because (reason), before / after / when (time).
Board: sunny, so we went hiking • kept going because I wanted to see the lake • When we got home, I was happy and tired
Exit Ticket — Quick Check
  • 1. Choose: I was tired, ____ I kept going. → but
  • 2. Answer: Why did Alex keep going? → Because he wanted to see the lake.
  • 3. Group the word 'so': add / reason / result / time → result
