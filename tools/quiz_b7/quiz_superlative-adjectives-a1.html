Superlative Adjectives
Grammar, Reading & Writing
Learning Objective (A1) — The student forms superlative adjectives correctly (the …-est for short adjectives, the most … for long ones, the best / the worst), never omits 'the', tells superlatives apart from comparatives, reads a short text for detail, and writes true superlative sentences about their own country and life.

G Reading Comprehension (10 points)   Score:
Read the text and answer the questions in complete sentences.
My favourite country to visit is Peru. Cusco is one of the most beautiful cities in the world, and the people there are some of the friendliest I have ever met. The mountains near Cusco are the highest I have ever seen. The food is the best part of the trip for me, especially the local dishes. Machu Picchu is the most famous place in Peru, but it is also the most crowded. The quietest time to visit is early in the morning.
1. What does the writer say about Cusco?
2. What is the best part of the trip for the writer?
3. What is the most famous place in Peru?
4. When is the quietest time to visit Machu Picchu?
