Countable & Uncountable Nouns
Grammar, Reading & Writing
Learning Objective (A1) — The student distinguishes countable from uncountable nouns, uses a / an with singular countable nouns, some in affirmatives and any in negatives and questions, reads a short text for detail, and writes accurate sentences about food using these forms.

G Reading Comprehension (12 points)   Score:
Read the text and answer the questions in complete sentences.
It is Saturday morning. Lucia and her mother are at the supermarket. They are buying food for a picnic. Lucia puts four apples and a bunch of bananas in the basket. Her mother buys some bread, some cheese and a bottle of orange juice. They also buy a box of cookies. Lucia wants some tomatoes for a salad. They have some rice at home, so they don't buy any. Finally, they get some milk for the evening. "We have everything!" says Lucia.
1. Where are Lucia and her mother?
2. How many apples does Lucia put in the basket?
3. What does her mother buy?
4. Do they buy any rice? Why / why not?
5. What does Lucia want for a salad?
