Conjunctions: If, Before, After, While, Because, When
Grammar, Reading & Writing
Learning Objective (A1) — The student chooses and uses if, before, after, while, because and when to join two ideas into one sentence with correct word order and comma use, keeps the present simple after if / when for the future, reads a short narrative for detail, and writes connected sentences about their own day.
Topic: Conjunctions – if, before, after, while, because, when Est. Time: 35–40 min
A Match the Use (12 points)   Score:
Write the letter of the correct use next to each conjunction.
1. if →
a. gives a reason
2. because →
b. a condition or possibility
3. before →
c. two actions at the same time
4. while →
d. something earlier
5. after →
e. a point in time
6. when →
f. something later
B Choose the Conjunction (12 points)   Score:
Circle the best conjunction.
1. I always wash my hands (before / because) I eat.
2. She was happy (while / because) she got good grades.
3. Call me (if / while) you get to the airport.
4. (While / After) I cook dinner, I listen to music.
5. We went home (because / after) the film finished.
6. (If / When) it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.

G Reading Comprehension (12 points)   Score:
Read the text and answer the questions in complete sentences.
Last Sunday, my family went to the beach. We left home early because the roads are quiet in the morning. While my dad was driving, we listened to music. When we arrived, we put up the umbrella and had a picnic. After lunch, we swam in the sea and built a sandcastle. I was a little cold, but I stayed in the water because it was fun. Before we went home, we took some photos. If the weather is good next weekend, we will go again.
1. Why did the family leave home early?
2. What did they do while dad was driving?
3. What did they do when they arrived?
4. Why did the writer stay in the water?
5. What will the family do if the weather is good next weekend?
