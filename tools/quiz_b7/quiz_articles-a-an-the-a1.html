Articles: A, An, The
Grammar, Reading & Writing
Learning Objective (A1) — The student uses a before a consonant sound and an before a vowel sound, uses the for specific, unique and superlative nouns, leaves out the article with general plural and uncountable nouns, reads a short text for detail, and writes accurate sentences about their own life using articles.
Topic: Articles – a, an, the Est. Time: 35–40 min

G Reading Comprehension (10 points)   Score:
Read the text and answer the questions in complete sentences.
Yesterday, Mia visited a new city with a friend. They took a train to the city centre. First, they went to a famous museum. It has a great collection of paintings. After that, they had lunch at a small restaurant near the river. In the afternoon, they walked around the park and saw a street performer. It was a wonderful day. Mia wants to go back to the city soon.
1. How did Mia and her friend travel to the city centre?
2. What did they do first?
3. Where did they have lunch?
4. What did they see in the park?
