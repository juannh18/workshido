Answer Key
Exercise 1 – Picture Sentences (Suggested Answers)
Open answers — accept any correct sentence that uses a, an, or the appropriately.
  • 1. Laptop → This is a laptop. / The laptop is on the desk.
  • 2. Cup of coffee → This is a cup of coffee. / The coffee is hot.
  • 3. Airport (Departures) → They are at the airport. / There is an airport in my city.
  • 4. Mountain lake → There is a lake near the mountains. / The lake is beautiful.
  • 5. Living room → There is a sofa in the living room. / The living room is cozy.
Exercise 2 – Write a, an, or the (10 items)
  • 1. The book on the table. (also accept A — first mention)
  • 2. An interesting movie.
  • 3. An umbrella in my bag.
  • 4. An honest person.
  • 5. The dog in the garden. (also accept A)
  • 6. A university in my city. (⚠ a, not an — "university" starts with a /j/ consonant sound)
  • 7. A good student.
  • 8. An orange on the plate.
  • 9. An old house near the park.
  • 10. A delicious cake.
These are sentence fragments to complete, so both a/an (general) and the (specific) are defensible in items 1 and 5 — accept either if the student can justify it.
Exercise 3 – Paragraph About Your Weekend (Model Answer)
Last weekend I went to a park with a friend. We saw an old man playing the guitar. Then we ate an ice cream and walked around the lake. It was a fantastic day!
Check that students use both a/an and the at least once, and that a/an matches the sound of the next word.
Teacher Tip
The trap of this worksheet is item 6: a university. Remind students the rule is about sound, not spelling — "university" starts with /juː/ (a consonant sound), so it takes a, while "honest" starts with a silent h (vowel sound), so it takes an. Drill both aloud.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ an university
✓ a university (/juː/ = consonant sound)
✗ a honest person
✓ an honest person (silent h)
✗ a umbrella
✓ an umbrella
✗ I went to a park. A park was big.
✓ I went to a park. The park was big. (second mention → the)
Teacher Extra Activity
Sound Sort Race (10 min)
Write 12 nouns on the board (umbrella, university, hour, house, orange, uniform, egg, European city, apple, hospital, honest person, ice cream). In pairs, students sort them into A / AN columns as fast as they can. Review and have the class pronounce each phrase.

Lesson Plan (45–50 minutes)
TimeStageProcedure
5 minWarm-UpSay "an apple, a banana, an egg..." around the class with fruit/object words; students continue the chain choosing a or an by sound.
8 minPresentationReview the Remember box: a + consonant sound, an + vowel sound, the + specific noun. Contrast the two traps aloud: a university vs. an honest person.
10 minControlled PracticeStudents complete Exercise 2 individually (10 fill-ins). Check as a class; when an item allows a or the, ask students to justify their choice (general or specific?).
10 minGuided PracticeIn pairs, students write sentences for the five pictures in Exercise 1. Each pair reads their best sentence aloud; the class checks the articles.
10 minProductionStudents write their weekend paragraph (Exercise 3) using both a/an and the. Circulate and correct sound-rule errors on the spot.
5 minWrap-UpQuick Sound Sort Race with 6 of the 12 nouns to close the lesson with the a/an sound rule.
Follow-Up / Homework Idea
Students find 5 objects at home and write one sentence per object using a/an for the first mention and the for the second ("I have a lamp. The lamp is on my desk."). Next class, they read one pair aloud.
For Extra Support Let weaker students say each Exercise 2 phrase aloud before writing — hearing the first sound of the noun makes the a/an choice concrete. For Exercise 3, accept 3 sentences instead of a full paragraph.
For an Extra Challenge Ask fast finishers to add two more sentences to their paragraph containing a university or an hour — the two sound-rule exceptions — used correctly.
