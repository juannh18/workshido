Exercise 2 – Categorize the Words
The word box gives 12 plain nouns (book, chair, tree, pen, building, car, desk, phone, window, keys, mountain, bag) with no pictures, so "near vs. far" is inherently a matter of the student's own imagined scene — there is no single objectively correct split. Accept any reasonable placement where the student can justify it. Suggested model (3 words per column):
This: pen, phone, keys
That: mountain, building, car
These: book, desk, window
Those: tree, chair, bag
Exercise 3 – Complete the Sentences (Choose the Correct Word)
Only item 2 has an explicit cue ("over there"). The rest default to the most natural reading; accept the alternative if justified.
  • 1. (This / That) is my new laptop.
  • 2. (These / Those) are my friends over there.
  • 3. (This / That) building is very tall.
  • 4. (These / Those) are my favorite books.
  • 5. (This / That) is a very beautiful flower.
  • 6. (These / Those) mountains are amazing!
  • 7. (This / That) is not my bag.
  • 8. (These / Those) cars are very fast.
Exercise 4 – Write the Demonstrative
  • 1. (basket of apples, close) These are fresh apples.
  • 2. (single tree, distant field) That is a big tree.
  • 3. (shoes, close) These are my shoes.
  • 4. (beach chairs, wide shot) Those are beach chairs.
  • 5. (phone, close) This is my phone.
  • 6. (wind turbines, distant landscape) Those are wind turbines.
Exercise 5 – Odd One Out
Row 4 has an error in the original worksheet: it lists "these, those, bikes, car" — TWO non-demonstrative words instead of one. Flagged below; both are acceptable answers until the worksheet is corrected.
  • 1. this, that, these, book
  • 2. those, these, tree, that
  • 3. this, pen, that, those
  • 4. these, those, bikes, car (2 odd words — worksheet error)
  • 5. that, these, those, building
  • 6. this, that, laptop, those
Exercise 6 – Pair Work: Ask and Answer (open)
Example given: "A: What is this? B: This is a camera." Suggested answers for items 1–5:
2. A: What are those? B: Those are hot air balloons.
3. A: What are these? B: These are cacti.
4. A: What is that? B: That is a lighthouse.
5. A: What are these? B: These are chairs.
Teacher Tip
For Ex 2, don't present it as having one "correct" answer — use it as a speaking activity where students explain their choices to a partner. For Ex 5 item 4, tell students in advance that this row has a printing error with two possible odd words, so nobody thinks they made a mistake for choosing either one. For Ex 6, model the example dialogue with a real classroom object before pairs practice with the pictures.

Teacher Extra Activity
Vocabulary Flashcard Sort (10 min)
Students cut out or draw 8 small pictures of objects. In pairs, they place each card "near" or "far" on the desk and say the sentence aloud ("This is a pen." / "That is a car."). Then they swap roles. Reinforces Ex 1 and Ex 4 vocabulary with a physical, kinesthetic activity.
