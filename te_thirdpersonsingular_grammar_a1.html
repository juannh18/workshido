Teaching Support
Teacher Tip
Activity 1's four pictures were chosen to model the four spelling rules directly: watches (-ch → -es) and studies (consonant+y → -ies) appear right where students apply the Form Focus table, so mistakes here are easy to trace back to a specific rule.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ He watch TV.
✓ He watches TV.
✗ She study every night.
✓ She studies every night.
✗ Does Tom plays tennis?
✓ Does Tom play tennis?
✗ My dog don't sleep here.
✓ My dog doesn't sleep here.
Extra Class Activity
Third Person Charades (10 min)

One student mimes an action (reading, watching TV, studying, walking); the class calls out the sentence using he/she + the correct -s/-es form.

Lesson Plan (35–40 minutes)
Level: A1 Skill: Grammar Topic: Present simple: he/she/it spelling rules Exercises: 4 (1–4) + Exit Ticket Est. Time: 35–40 minutes
Materials & Prep
Printed worksheet copies (1 per student) Colored pencils (for the Activity 1 pictures)
TimeStageProcedure
6 minWarm-UpRead the Form Focus table together, then the four spelling-rule categories underneath.
8 minLook, Color and Complete (1)Students write the correct verb form for each picture, then color the illustrations.
7 minChoose the Correct Form (2)Students circle the correct option in 6 sentences.
6 minMake Them Negative (3)Students rewrite 3 sentences with doesn't.
6 minWrite the Questions (4)Students form 4 questions with Does.
4 minExit TicketStudents write one affirmative, one negative and one question sentence.
Follow-Up / Homework Idea

Homework: write 4 sentences describing what a pet or family member does every day, using correct -s/-es spelling.

For Extra Support

Before Activity 2, have students sort the 6 verbs (cook, like, play, stop, have, give) into the four spelling-rule groups from the Form Focus table so they apply the right rule before choosing.

For an Extra Challenge

Students write two more sentences using irregular third-person forms (has, does, goes) about someone they know.

Extension & Fast-Finisher Ideas
Name a verb that needs -es because it ends in -sh. Write the third-person form of 'try'. Ask a partner a does-question about their best friend. Find one more consonant+y verb and write its -ies form.
Key Language & Board Notes
Target: He/She/It + verb-s/-es (present simple third person), Does + subject + base verb? for questions.
Board: He works every day. She doesn't study French. Does it go fast?
Exit Ticket — Quick Check
  • 1. Third-person form of 'watch'? → watches
  • 2. Third-person form of 'study'? → studies
  • 3. Third-person form of 'have'? → has
