Exercise 1 – Choose the Correct Imperative

2. Don't eat in class.
3. Be on time.
4. Please help me.

6. Don't run here.

Exercise 2 – Match the Imperative with the Picture
1. Don't touch the dog. → a
2. Please turn off your phone. → b
3. Open the window. → c
4. Be quiet. → d
5. Wash your hands. → e
Exercise 3 – Complete the Sentences
1. Open the book.
2. Wash your hands before eating.
3. Do your homework every day.
4. Don't be late to class.
5. Listen to music loudly.
6. Turn off the TV before bed.
7. Ask questions in class.
8. Don't run in the hallway.
9. Keep neat and tidy.
10. Bring your lunch with you.
Exercise 4 – Put the Words in Order
  • 1. Close the door.
  • 2. Don't talk in class.
  • 3. Put your toys away.
  • 4. Please take a seat.
  • 5. Don't be rude.
Exercise 5 – Rewrite the Sentences (negative imperative)
Example given: Eat your vegetables. → Don't eat your vegetables.
  • 1. Don't be late.
  • 2. Don't use your phone in class.
  • 3. Don't forget your homework.
  • 4. Don't leave your trash on the floor.
  • 5. Don't shout in the library.
Exercise 6 – Write Your Own Imperatives (open)
Accept any correct imperative per situation. Suggested models:
1. You're at school → Listen to the teacher. 2. It's your friend's birthday → Have a great day! 3. It's raining → Take an umbrella. 4. It's time to eat → Wash your hands. 5. You care about the environment → Recycle your trash.
Exercise 7 – Choose the Best Imperative
1. Be quiet in the library. → a
2. Friend to come here. → a
3. Someone not to enter. → a
4. Someone to help you. → a
5. Tell someone to study. → a
Option (a) is always the correct base-form imperative; (b) wrongly adds a subject or -s, (c) is grammatically incomplete or awkward.
Teacher Tip
Start with the Rules box and Examples on the worksheet — drill each rule chorally with a real classroom action (e.g., actually standing up for "Stand up"). For Ex 7, point out that every correct answer is option (a) so students learn to recognize the base-form pattern itself, not just memorize letters.
Common Student Mistakes
✗ Closes the door.
✓ Close the door.
✗ You be quiet.
✓ Be quiet. (no subject)
✗ Not eat in class.
✓ Don't eat in class.
✗ Please helps me.
✓ Please help me.
Teacher Extra Activity
Simon Says (10 min)
Classic TPR game: call out imperatives ("Touch your nose." / "Don't sit down.") — students only follow the command if it starts with "Simon says." Reinforces both affirmative and negative imperatives physically and playfully.
